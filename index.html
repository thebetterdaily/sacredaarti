( The evening aarti )

Light the lamp and begin with Ganesha, who is always worshipped first. Then sing to the deity of the day: Shiva on Monday, Hanuman on Tuesday, Lakshmi on Friday, Shani Dev on Saturday. Close with Om Jai Jagdish Hare, offer the flame to everyone, and share the prasad.

Keep it on your phone

Add Sacred Aarti to your home screen like an app. It is free and opens even without internet.

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open sacredaarti.com in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow ↑).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add.

Android

  1. Open sacredaarti.com in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
  4. Tap Install.

Once added, every aarti is saved on your phone, so it still opens in the temple or on a plane with no signal. Show me how
