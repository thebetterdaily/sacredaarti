49 · SHLOKA

Guru Brahma Guru Vishnu

गुरुर्ब्रह्मा गुरुर्विष्णुः

UniversalDeity
ShlokaForm
Daily, ThursdayDay
Guru Purnima, Start of studyOccasion
ॐ

A verse of reverence for the teacher, traditionally recited before study and on Guru Purnima.

गुरुर्ब्रह्मा गुरुर्विष्णुः गुरुर्देवो महेश्वरः।
गुरुः साक्षात् परब्रह्म तस्मै श्रीगुरवे नमः॥

Gururbrahmā gururvishnuh gururdevo maheshvarah |
Guruh sākshāt parabrahma tasmai shrīgurave namah ||

The Guru is Brahma, the Guru is Bhagwan Vishnu, the Guru is Lord Maheshvara; the Guru is truly the Supreme Brahman. Salutations to that revered Guru.
