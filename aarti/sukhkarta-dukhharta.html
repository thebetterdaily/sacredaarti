09 · AARTI

Sukhkarta Dukhharta

सुखकर्ता दुःखहर्ता

GaneshaDeity
AartiForm
WednesdayDay
Ganesh ChaturthiOccasion

A Marathi aarti composed by the 17th-century saint Samarth Ramdas, who signs it as 'Das Ramacha' (servant of Rama). It is the signature aarti of Ganesh Chaturthi in Maharashtra and is now sung across India and abroad.

सुखकर्ता दुःखहर्ता वार्ता विघ्नाची।
नुरवी पुरवी प्रेम कृपा जयाची॥
सर्वांगी सुंदर उटी शेंदुराची।
कंठी झळके माळ मुक्ताफळांची॥

Sukhkartā duhkhhartā vārtā vighnāchī |
Nuravī puravī prem kripā jayāchī ||
Sarvāngī sundar utī shendurāchī |
Kanthī jhalake māl muktāphalānchī ||

He brings joy and removes sorrow, and leaves no trace of obstacles; his love and grace are ever present. His whole body glows with a paste of vermilion, and a necklace of pearls shines at his throat.

॥ जय देव जय देव जय मंगलमूर्ती। दर्शनमात्रे मन कामना पुरती ॥Jai dev jai dev jai mangalamūrtī | Darshanamātre man kāmanā puratī

रत्नखचित फरा तुज गौरीकुमरा।
चंदनाची उटी कुंकुमकेशरा॥
हिरेजडित मुकुट शोभतो बरा।
रुणझुणती नूपुरे चरणी घागरिया॥

Ratnakhachit pharā tuj gaurīkumarā |
Chandanāchī utī kunkumakesharā ||
Hirejadit mukut shobhato barā |
Runajhunatī nūpure charanī ghāgariyā ||

O son of Gauri, a jewelled seat is set for you, with sandal paste, kumkum and saffron. A diamond-studded crown suits you well, and anklets chime at your feet.

॥ जय देव जय देव जय मंगलमूर्ती। दर्शनमात्रे मन कामना पुरती ॥Jai dev jai dev jai mangalamūrtī | Darshanamātre man kāmanā puratī

लंबोदर पीतांबर फणिवरबंधना।
सरळ सोंड वक्रतुंड त्रिनयना॥
दास रामाचा वाट पाहे सदना।
संकटी पावावे निर्वाणी रक्षावे सुरवरवंदना॥

Lambodar pītāmbar phanivarabandhanā |
Saral sond vakratund trinayanā ||
Dās rāmāchā vāt pāhe sadanā |
Sankatī pāvāve nirvānī rakshāve suravaravandanā ||

Big-bellied Lord in yellow silk, with a serpent for a belt, a curved trunk and three eyes: Rama's servant waits for you at home. Come to us in hard times and protect us at the end, O Lord honoured by the gods.

॥ जय देव जय देव जय मंगलमूर्ती। दर्शनमात्रे मन कामना पुरती ॥Jai dev jai dev jai mangalamūrtī | Darshanamātre man kāmanā puratī

The refrain ॥ जय देव जय देव जय मंगलमूर्ती। दर्शनमात्रे मन कामना पुरती ॥ is sung after every stanza, as marked.
