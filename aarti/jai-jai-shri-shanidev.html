31 · AARTI

Jai Jai Shri Shanidev

जय जय श्री शनिदेव

Shani DevDeity
AartiForm
SaturdayDay
Shani Jayanti, Shani AmavasyaOccasion

Sung on Saturdays, usually with a lamp of sesame or mustard oil. Shani Dev, son of the Sun and of Chhaya, is the planet of discipline and justice; devotees pray to him for relief during difficult periods such as Sade Sati.

जय जय श्री शनिदेव भक्तन हितकारी।
सूरज के पुत्र प्रभु छाया महतारी॥

Jai jai shrī Shanidev bhaktan hitkārī |
Sūraj ke putra prabhu chhāyā mahtārī ||

Glory to Shri Shanidev, well-wisher of devotees, son of the Sun, whose mother is Chhaya.

श्याम अंक वक्र दृष्टि चतुर्भुजा धारी।
नीलाम्बर धार नाथ गज की असवारी॥

Shyām ank vakra drishti chaturbhujā dhārī |
Nīlāmbar dhār nāth gaj kī asvārī ||

Dark-bodied, with a sidelong gaze and four arms, the Lord is dressed in blue and rides an elephant.

॥ जय जय श्री शनिदेव ॥Jai jai shrī Shanidev

किरीट मुकुट शीश सहज दिपत है लिलारी।
मुक्तन की माल गले शोभित बलिहारी॥

Kirīt mukut shīsh sahaj dipat hai lilārī |
Muktan kī māl gale shobhit balihārī ||

A crown rests naturally on his head and his forehead glows; a garland of pearls graces his neck. I offer myself to him.

॥ जय जय श्री शनिदेव ॥Jai jai shrī Shanidev

मोदक और मिष्ठान चढ़े, चढ़ती पान सुपारी।
लोहा तिल तेल उड़द महिषी है अति प्यारी॥

Modak aur mishthān chadhe, chadhtī pān supārī |
Lohā til tel udad mahishī hai ati pyārī ||

Sweets, betel leaf and areca nut are offered to him; iron, sesame, oil, black urad and the buffalo are dear to him.

॥ जय जय श्री शनिदेव ॥Jai jai shrī Shanidev

देव दनुज ऋषि मुनि सुमिरत नर नारी।
विश्वनाथ धरत ध्यान हम हैं शरण तुम्हारी॥

Dev danuj rishi muni sumirat nar nārī |
Vishvanāth dharat dhyān ham hain sharan tumhārī ||

Gods, demons, sages, men and women all remember you; even Vishwanath meditates on you. We take refuge in you.

॥ जय जय श्री शनिदेव ॥Jai jai shrī Shanidev

The refrain ॥ जय जय श्री शनिदेव ॥ is sung after every stanza, as marked.
