16 · AARTI

Om Jai Shri Shyam Hare (Khatu Shyam Aarti)

ॐ जय श्री श्याम हरे

Khatu ShyamDeity
AartiForm
Ekadashi, SundayDay
Phalgun Mela, EkadashiOccasion

The aarti of Baba Khatu Shyam, worshipped at Khatu in Rajasthan as Krishna in the form of Barbarika, the grandson of Bhima who offered his head before the Mahabharata war. It is sung at the Khatu Shyam temple and at Shyam bhajan evenings across North India, especially on Ekadashi.

ॐ जय श्री श्याम हरे, बाबा जय श्री श्याम हरे।
खाटू धाम विराजत, अनुपम रूप धरे॥

Om jai shrī Shyām hare, bābā jai shrī Shyām hare |
Khātū dhām virājat, anupam rūp dhare ||

Victory to Shri Shyam, O Baba Shyam, who sits in his home at Khatu in a form beyond compare.

रतन जड़ित सिंहासन, सिर पर चंवर ढुरे।
तन केसरिया बागो, कुण्डल श्रवण पड़े॥

Ratan jadit sinhāsan, sir par chanvar dhure |
Tan kesariyā bāgo, kundal shravan pade ||

He sits on a jewelled throne with a whisk waved over his head; he wears a saffron robe, with earrings in his ears.

॥ ॐ जय श्री श्याम हरे, बाबा जय श्री श्याम हरे ॥Om jai shrī Shyām hare, bābā jai shrī Shyām hare

गल पुष्पों की माला, सिर पर मुकुट धरे।
खेवत धूप अग्नि पर, दीपक ज्योति जले॥

Gal pushpon kī mālā, sir par mukut dhare |
Khevat dhūp agni par, dīpak jyoti jale ||

A garland of flowers hangs at his neck and a crown sits on his head; incense smoulders on the coals and the lamp burns bright.

॥ ॐ जय श्री श्याम हरे, बाबा जय श्री श्याम हरे ॥Om jai shrī Shyām hare, bābā jai shrī Shyām hare

मोदक खीर चूरमा, सुवरण थाल भरे।
सेवक भोग लगावत, सेवा नित्य करे॥

Modak khīr chūrmā, suvran thāl bhare |
Sevak bhog lagāvat, sevā nitya kare ||

Golden plates are filled with modak, kheer and churma; his servants offer them to him and serve him every day.

॥ ॐ जय श्री श्याम हरे, बाबा जय श्री श्याम हरे ॥Om jai shrī Shyām hare, bābā jai shrī Shyām hare

झांझ कटोरा और घड़ियावल, शंख मृदंग घुरे।
भक्त आरती गावे, जय-जयकार करे॥

Jhānjh katorā aur ghadiyāval, shankh mridang ghure |
Bhakt ārtī gāve, jai-jaykār kare ||

Cymbals, bells and gongs ring out with the conch and the drum, as devotees sing the aarti and cry out his victory.

॥ ॐ जय श्री श्याम हरे, बाबा जय श्री श्याम हरे ॥Om jai shrī Shyām hare, bābā jai shrī Shyām hare

जो ध्यावे फल पावे, सब दुःख से उबरे।
सेवक जन निज मुख से, श्री श्याम-श्याम उचरे॥

Jo dhyāve phal pāve, sab dukh se ubre |
Sevak jan nij mukh se, shrī Shyām-Shyām uchre ||

Whoever meditates on him receives the fruit and is freed from every sorrow; his devotees keep the name 'Shyam, Shyam' on their lips.

॥ ॐ जय श्री श्याम हरे, बाबा जय श्री श्याम हरे ॥Om jai shrī Shyām hare, bābā jai shrī Shyām hare

श्री श्याम बिहारी जी की आरती, जो कोई नर गावे।
कहत भक्त-जन, मनवांछित फल पावे॥

Shrī Shyām bihārī jī kī ārtī, jo koī nar gāve |
Kahat bhakt-jan, manvānchhit phal pāve ||

Whoever sings this aarti of Shri Shyam Bihari, say the devotees, receives what the heart desires.

॥ ॐ जय श्री श्याम हरे, बाबा जय श्री श्याम हरे ॥Om jai shrī Shyām hare, bābā jai shrī Shyām hare

जय श्री श्याम हरे, बाबा जी श्री श्याम हरे।
निज भक्तों के तुमने, पूरण काज करे॥

Jai shrī Shyām hare, bābā jī shrī Shyām hare |
Nij bhakton ke tumne, pūran kāj kare ||

Victory to Shri Shyam, O revered Baba: you have fulfilled every task of your devotees.

॥ ॐ जय श्री श्याम हरे, बाबा जय श्री श्याम हरे ॥Om jai shrī Shyām hare, bābā jai shrī Shyām hare

The refrain ॥ ॐ जय श्री श्याम हरे, बाबा जय श्री श्याम हरे ॥ is sung after every stanza, as marked.
