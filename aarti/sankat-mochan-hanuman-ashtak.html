Sacred Aarti / Hanumanji / Stuti

14 · STUTI

Sankat Mochan Hanuman Ashtak

संकटमोचन हनुमानाष्टक

HanumanjiDeity
StutiForm
Tuesday, SaturdayDay
Hanuman Jayanti, TuesdayOccasion

Eight verses by Goswami Tulsidas recalling the times Hanumanji rescued others from danger: freeing the sun, finding Sita Mata, bringing the Sanjivani herb, and rescuing Shri Ram and Lakshmana from Ahiravana. Each verse ends with the line that gives the hymn its name, 'who does not know your name, Sankat Mochan, the remover of troubles?' It is recited after the Hanuman Chalisa on Tuesdays and Saturdays.

1

बाल समय रबि भक्षि लियो तब, तीनहुँ लोक भयो अँधियारो।
ताहि सों त्रास भयो जग को, यह संकट काहु सों जात न टारो॥
देवन आनि करी बिनती तब, छाँड़ि दियो रबि कष्ट निवारो।

Bāl samay rabi bhakshi liyo tab, tīnhun lok bhayo andhiyāro |
Tāhi son trās bhayo jag ko, yah sankat kāhu son jāt na tāro ||
Devan āni karī bintī tab, chhāndi diyo rabi kasht nivāro |

As a child you swallowed the sun, and darkness fell on the three worlds. The whole world was terrified, and no one could end the crisis. When the gods came and pleaded with you, you released the sun and ended their distress.

॥ को नहिं जानत है जग में कपि संकटमोचन नाम तिहारो ॥Ko nahin jānat hai jag mein kapi sankatmochan nām tihāro

2

बालि की त्रास कपीस बसै गिरि, जात महाप्रभु पंथ निहारो।
चौंकि महामुनि साप दियो तब, चाहिय कौन बिचार बिचारो॥
कै द्विज रूप लिवाय महाप्रभु, सो तुम दास के सोक निवारो।

Bāli kī trās kapīs basai giri, jāt mahāprabhu panth nihāro |
Chaunki mahāmuni sāp diyo tab, chāhiy kaun bichār bichāro ||
Kai dvij rūp livāy mahāprabhu, so tum dās ke sok nivāro |

Fearing Bali, Sugriva lived on the mountain, watching the road for the Lord. Startled, he wondered what to do. Taking the form of a Brahmin, you brought the Lord to him, and so ended your servant's grief.

॥ को नहिं जानत है जग में कपि संकटमोचन नाम तिहारो ॥Ko nahin jānat hai jag mein kapi sankatmochan nām tihāro

3

अंगद के संग लेन गए सिय, खोज कपीस यह बैन उचारो।
जीवत ना बचिहौ हम सो जु, बिना सुधि लाये इहाँ पगु धारो॥
हेरि थके तट सिन्धु सबै तब, लाय सिया-सुधि प्रान उबारो।

Angad ke sang len gae siy, khoj kapīs yah bain uchāro |
Jīvat nā bachihau ham so ju, binā sudhi lāye ihān pagu dhāro ||
Heri thake tat sindhu sabai tab, lāy siyā-sudhi prān ubāro |

You went with Angada to search for Sita Mata, and Sugriva declared: 'Whoever returns without news of her will not stay alive.' When everyone had searched until they were exhausted on the seashore, you brought news of Sita Mata and saved their lives.

॥ को नहिं जानत है जग में कपि संकटमोचन नाम तिहारो ॥Ko nahin jānat hai jag mein kapi sankatmochan nām tihāro

4

रावन त्रास दई सिय को सब, राक्षसि सों कहि सोक निवारो।
ताहि समय हनुमान महाप्रभु, जाय महा रजनीचर मारो॥
चाहत सीय असोक सों आगि सु, दै प्रभु मुद्रिका सोक निवारो।

Rāvan trās daī siy ko sab, rākshasi son kahi sok nivāro |
Tāhi samay Hanumān mahāprabhu, jāy mahā rajnīchar māro ||
Chāhat sīy asok son āgi su, dai prabhu mudrikā sok nivāro |

Ravana tormented Sita Mata and ordered the demonesses to break her spirit. At that time, great Hanumanji, you went and slew mighty demons. When Sita longed for fire from the Ashoka tree, you gave her the Lord's ring and ended her grief.

॥ को नहिं जानत है जग में कपि संकटमोचन नाम तिहारो ॥Ko nahin jānat hai jag mein kapi sankatmochan nām tihāro

5

बान लग्यो उर लछिमन के तब, प्रान तजे सुत रावन मारो।
लै गृह बैद्य सुषेन समेत, तबै गिरि द्रोन सु बीर उपारो॥
आनि सजीवन हाथ दई तब, लछिमन के तुम प्रान उबारो।

Bān lagyo ur Lachhiman ke tab, prān taje sut Rāvan māro |
Lai grih baidya Sushen samet, tabai giri dron su bīr upāro ||
Āni sajīvan hāth daī tab, Lachhiman ke tum prān ubāro |

When Ravana's son struck Lakshmana in the chest with an arrow and his life was ebbing, you brought the physician Sushena with his house, and the hero uprooted the Drona mountain. You brought the Sanjivani herb, placed it in their hands, and saved Lakshmana's life.

॥ को नहिं जानत है जग में कपि संकटमोचन नाम तिहारो ॥Ko nahin jānat hai jag mein kapi sankatmochan nām tihāro

6

रावन युद्ध अजान कियो तब, नाग कि फाँस सबै सिर डारो।
श्रीरघुनाथ समेत सबै दल, मोह भयो यह संकट भारो॥
आनि खगेस तबै हनुमान जु, बंधन काटि सुत्रास निवारो।

Rāvan yuddh ajān kiyo tab, nāg ki phāns sabai sir dāro |
Shrīraghunāth samet sabai dal, moh bhayo yah sankat bhāro ||
Āni khages tabai Hanumān ju, bandhan kāti sutrās nivāro |

When Ravana fought by trickery and cast the serpent noose over everyone, Shri Ram and the whole army fell senseless in this grave crisis. Then you brought Garuda, king of birds, who cut the bonds and ended the terror.

॥ को नहिं जानत है जग में कपि संकटमोचन नाम तिहारो ॥Ko nahin jānat hai jag mein kapi sankatmochan nām tihāro

7

बंधु समेत जबै अहिरावन, लै रघुनाथ पताल सिधारो।
देबिहिं पूजि भली बिधि सों बलि, देउ सबै मिलि मंत्र बिचारो॥
जाय सहाय भयो तब ही, अहिरावन सैन्य समेत सँहारो।

Bandhu samet jabai Ahirāvan, lai Raghunāth patāl sidhāro |
Debihin pūji bhalī bidhi son bali, deu sabai mili mantra bichāro ||
Jāy sahāy bhayo tab hī, Ahirāvan sainya samet sanhāro |

When Ahiravana carried Shri Ram and his brother away to the underworld, and planned with his followers to offer them as a sacrifice to the goddess, you went to their aid and destroyed Ahiravana with his army.

॥ को नहिं जानत है जग में कपि संकटमोचन नाम तिहारो ॥Ko nahin jānat hai jag mein kapi sankatmochan nām tihāro

8

काज किये बड़ देवन के तुम, बीर महाप्रभु देखि बिचारो।
कौन सो संकट मोर गरीब को, जो तुमसे नहिं जात है टारो॥
बेगि हरो हनुमान महाप्रभु, जो कछु संकट होय हमारो।

Kāj kiye bad devan ke tum, bīr mahāprabhu dekhi bichāro |
Kaun so sankat mor garīb ko, jo tumse nahin jāt hai tāro ||
Begi haro Hanumān mahāprabhu, jo kachhu sankat hoy hamāro |

You have done great deeds even for the gods; consider this, great hero. What trouble of mine, poor as I am, is there that you cannot remove? Quickly take away, great Hanumanji, whatever trouble we have.

॥ को नहिं जानत है जग में कपि संकटमोचन नाम तिहारो ॥Ko nahin jānat hai jag mein kapi sankatmochan nām tihāro

Doha

लाल देह लाली लसे, अरु धरि लाल लँगूर।
बज्र देह दानव दलन, जय जय जय कपि सूर॥

Lāl deh lālī lase, aru dhari lāl langūr |
Bajra deh dānav dalan, jai jai jai kapi sūr ||

Your red body glows with a red radiance, and your tail too is red. Thunderbolt-bodied destroyer of demons, victory, victory, victory to the heroic monkey.

The refrain ॥ को नहिं जानत है जग में कपि संकटमोचन नाम तिहारो ॥ is sung after every stanza, as marked.
