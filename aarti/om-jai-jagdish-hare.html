02 · AARTI

Om Jai Jagdish Hare

ॐ जय जगदीश हरे

VishnuDeity
AartiForm
Thursday, Daily PujaDay
Daily Puja, DiwaliOccasion

Written around 1870 by Pandit Shardha Ram Phillauri of Punjab, this is the most widely sung aarti in the world and usually closes any puja, whichever deity is worshipped. Many families add the final 'Tan man dhan' stanza; a common sung variant of stanza 4 is 'Main moorakh phalkami, main sevak tum swami'.

ॐ जय जगदीश हरे, स्वामी जय जगदीश हरे।
भक्त जनों के संकट, दास जनों के संकट, क्षण में दूर करे॥

Om jai Jagdīsh hare, swāmī jai Jagdīsh hare |
Bhakt janon ke sankat, dās janon ke sankat, kshan mein dūr kare ||

Glory to you, Lord of the universe, O Hari, who removes the troubles of your devotees in an instant.

जो ध्यावे फल पावे, दुःख बिनसे मन का।
सुख सम्पति घर आवे, कष्ट मिटे तन का॥

Jo dhyāve phal pāve, dukh binse man kā |
Sukh sampati ghar āve, kasht mite tan kā ||

Whoever meditates on you is rewarded: sorrow leaves the mind, happiness and prosperity come home and the body's pain is eased.

॥ ॐ जय जगदीश हरे ॥Om jai Jagdīsh hare

मात पिता तुम मेरे, शरण गहूँ किसकी।
तुम बिन और न दूजा, आस करूँ जिसकी॥

Māt pitā tum mere, sharan gahūn kiskī |
Tum bin aur na dūjā, ās karūn jiskī ||

You are my mother and father; whose shelter could I seek but yours? Without you there is no one else I can hope in.

॥ ॐ जय जगदीश हरे ॥Om jai Jagdīsh hare

तुम पूरण परमात्मा, तुम अन्तर्यामी।
पारब्रह्म परमेश्वर, तुम सब के स्वामी॥

Tum pūran parmātmā, tum antaryāmī |
Pārbrahm parmeshvar, tum sab ke swāmī ||

You are the complete Supreme Self, the one who dwells within all; the transcendent Brahman, the Supreme Lord, master of all.

॥ ॐ जय जगदीश हरे ॥Om jai Jagdīsh hare

तुम करुणा के सागर, तुम पालनकर्ता।
मैं मूरख खल कामी, कृपा करो भर्ता॥

Tum karunā ke sāgar, tum pālankartā |
Main mūrakh khal kāmī, kripā karo bhartā ||

You are an ocean of compassion and the sustainer of all. I am foolish and full of desire; have mercy on me, O Lord.

॥ ॐ जय जगदीश हरे ॥Om jai Jagdīsh hare

तुम हो एक अगोचर, सबके प्राणपति।
किस विधि मिलूँ दयामय, तुमको मैं कुमति॥

Tum ho ek agochar, sabke prānpati |
Kis vidhi milūn dayāmay, tumko main kumati ||

You are the One beyond the senses, the lord of every life. O merciful one, how can someone as unwise as I reach you?

॥ ॐ जय जगदीश हरे ॥Om jai Jagdīsh hare

दीनबन्धु दुखहर्ता, ठाकुर तुम मेरे।
अपने हाथ उठाओ, द्वार पड़ा तेरे॥

Dīnbandhu dukhhartā, thākur tum mere |
Apne hāth uthāo, dvār padā tere ||

Friend of the humble and remover of sorrow, you are my master. Raise your hand in blessing; I lie at your door.

॥ ॐ जय जगदीश हरे ॥Om jai Jagdīsh hare

विषय विकार मिटाओ, पाप हरो देवा।
श्रद्धा भक्ति बढ़ाओ, सन्तन की सेवा॥

Vishay vikār mitāo, pāp haro devā |
Shraddhā bhakti badhāo, santan kī sevā ||

Wipe away worldly cravings and take away my sins, O Lord. Increase my faith, my devotion and my service to the saints.

॥ ॐ जय जगदीश हरे ॥Om jai Jagdīsh hare

तन मन धन सब है तेरा, सब कुछ है तेरा।
तेरा तुझको अर्पण, क्या लागे मेरा॥

Tan man dhan sab hai terā, sab kuchh hai terā |
Terā tujhko arpan, kyā lāge merā ||

Body, mind and wealth are all yours; everything is yours. I offer you only what is already yours; what is mine to give?

॥ ॐ जय जगदीश हरे ॥Om jai Jagdīsh hare

The refrain ॥ ॐ जय जगदीश हरे ॥ is sung after every stanza, as marked.
