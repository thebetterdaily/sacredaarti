Aarti Shri Ramayan Ji Ki

आरती श्री रामायण जी की

RamDeity
AartiForm
DailyDay
Ramayan Path, Ram NavamiOccasion

The aarti of the Ramayan itself, sung at the end of a Ramcharitmanas or Sundar Kand recitation. It is in Tulsidas's Awadhi and names the sages, gods and poets who have sung Rama's story.

आरती श्री रामायण जी की।
कीरति कलित ललित सिय पी की॥

Ārtī shrī Rāmāyan jī kī |
Kīrti kalit lalit siy pī kī ||

Aarti to the holy Ramayan, the graceful and lovely glory of Sita's beloved.

गावत ब्रह्मादिक मुनि नारद।
बालमीक बिग्यान बिसारद॥
सुक सनकादि सेष अरु सारद।
बरनि पवनसुत कीरति नीकी॥

Gāvat brahmādik muni Nārad |
Bālmīk bigyān bisārad ||
Suk sankādi sesh aru Sārad |
Barni pavansut kīrti nīkī ||

Brahma and the gods, the sage Narada and Valmiki, master of wisdom, sing it; Shuka, Sanaka and his brothers, Shesha and Sharada sing it, and Hanuman, son of the Wind, describes its beautiful glory.

॥ आरती श्री रामायण जी की। कीरति कलित ललित सिय पी की ॥Ārtī shrī Rāmāyan jī kī | Kīrti kalit lalit siy pī kī

गावत बेद पुरान अष्टदस।
छओं सास्त्र सब ग्रंथन को रस॥
मुनि जन धन संतन को सरबस।
सार अंस संमत सबही की॥

Gāvat bed purān ashtadas |
Chhaon sāstra sab granthan ko ras ||
Muni jan dhan santan ko sarbas |
Sār ans sammat sabahī kī ||

The Vedas and the eighteen Puranas sing it; it is the essence of the six schools and of every scripture. It is the wealth of sages and the everything of saints, the core that all agree on.

॥ आरती श्री रामायण जी की। कीरति कलित ललित सिय पी की ॥Ārtī shrī Rāmāyan jī kī | Kīrti kalit lalit siy pī kī

गावत संतत संभु भवानी।
अरु घटसंभव मुनि बिग्यानी॥
ब्यास आदि कबिबर्ज बखानी।
कागभुसुंडि गरुड़ के ही की॥

Gāvat santat sambhu Bhavānī |
Aru ghatsambhav muni bigyānī ||
Byās ādi kabibarj bakhānī |
Kāgbhusundi garud ke hī kī ||

Shiva and Bhavani sing it without end, as does the wise sage Agastya; Vyasa and the great poets have told it, and it is dear to the hearts of Kakbhushundi and Garuda.

॥ आरती श्री रामायण जी की। कीरति कलित ललित सिय पी की ॥Ārtī shrī Rāmāyan jī kī | Kīrti kalit lalit siy pī kī

कलिमल हरनि बिषय रस फीकी।
सुभग सिंगार मुक्ति जुबती की॥
दलन रोग भव मूरि अमी की।
तात मात सब बिधि तुलसी की॥

Kalimal harni bishay ras phīkī |
Subhag singār mukti jubtī kī ||
Dalan rog bhav mūri amī kī |
Tāt māt sab bidhi Tulsī kī ||

It washes away the stains of the dark age and makes the pleasures of the senses taste dull; it is the lovely adornment of liberation. It is the nectar-root that destroys the disease of worldly life, and in every way it is father and mother to Tulsi.

॥ आरती श्री रामायण जी की। कीरति कलित ललित सिय पी की ॥Ārtī shrī Rāmāyan jī kī | Kīrti kalit lalit siy pī kī

The refrain ॥ आरती श्री रामायण जी की। कीरति कलित ललित सिय पी की ॥ is sung after every stanza, as marked.
