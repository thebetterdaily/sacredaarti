31 · MANTRA

Shanti Path (Dyauh Shanti)

शान्ति पाठ

UniversalDeity
MantraForm
DailyDay
End of havan or pujaOccasion
ॐ

From the Shukla Yajurveda (36.17). It is chanted at the close of a havan or puja, praying for peace in every part of creation.

ॐ द्यौः शान्तिरन्तरिक्षं शान्तिः पृथिवी शान्तिरापः शान्तिरोषधयः शान्तिः।
वनस्पतयः शान्तिर्विश्वेदेवाः शान्तिर्ब्रह्म शान्तिः सर्वं शान्तिः शान्तिरेव शान्तिः सा मा शान्तिरेधि॥
ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः॥

Om dyauh shāntirantariksham shāntih prithivī shāntirāpah shāntiroshadhayah shāntih |
Vanaspatayah shāntirvishvedevāh shāntirbrahma shāntih sarvam shāntih shāntireva shāntih sā mā shāntiredhi ||
Om shāntih shāntih shāntih ||

May there be peace in the heavens, peace in the sky, peace on earth, peace in the waters, peace in the healing plants; peace in the trees, peace among all the gods, peace in Brahman, peace in everything; peace itself, and may that peace come to me. Om, peace, peace, peace.
