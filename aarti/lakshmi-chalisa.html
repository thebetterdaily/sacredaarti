18 · CHALISA

Shri Lakshmi Chalisa

श्री लक्ष्मी चालीसा

LakshmiDeity
ChalisaForm
FridayDay
Diwali, DhanterasOccasion

Forty verses to Lakshmi, goddess of wealth and good fortune, recalling her rising from the churning of the ocean and her life beside Vishnu in every avatar, as Sita with Rama and Rukmini with Krishna. It is recited on Fridays and during the Diwali season. The closing doha carries the poet's name, Ramdas.

Doha

मातु लक्ष्मी करि कृपा, करो हृदय में वास।
मनोकामना सिद्ध करि, पुरवहु मेरी आस॥

Mātu Lakshmī kari kripā, karo hriday mein vās |
Manokāmnā siddh kari, puravhu merī ās ||

Mother Lakshmi, be gracious and dwell in my heart; fulfil my wishes and complete my hopes.

Soratha

यही मोर अरदास, हाथ जोड़ विनती करूँ।
सब विधि करौ सुवास, जय जननि जगदंबिका॥

Yahī mor ardās, hāth jod vintī karūn |
Sab vidhi karau suvās, jai janani jagdambikā ||

This is my plea, made with folded hands: make your home with us in every way, victory to you, Mother of the world.

Chaupai 1–4

सिन्धु सुता मैं सुमिरौं तोही। ज्ञान बुद्धि विद्या दो मोही॥
तुम समान नहिं कोई उपकारी। सब विधि पुरवहु आस हमारी॥
जय जय जगत जननि जगदम्बा। सबकी तुम ही हो अवलम्बा॥
तुम ही हो सब घट घट वासी। विनती यही हमारी खासी॥

Sindhu sutā main sumiraun tohī | Gyān buddhi vidyā do mohī ||
Tum samān nahin koī upkārī | Sab vidhi puravhu ās hamārī ||
Jai jai jagat janani Jagdambā | Sabkī tum hī ho avlambā ||
Tum hī ho sab ghat ghat vāsī | Vintī yahī hamārī khāsī ||

Daughter of the ocean, I remember you: give me knowledge, understanding and learning. No one is as generous as you; fulfil our hopes in every way. Victory to the Mother of the world, the support of all, who lives in every heart: this is our special prayer.

Chaupai 5–8

जगजननी जय सिन्धु कुमारी। दीनन की तुम हो हितकारी॥
विनवौं नित्य तुमहिं महारानी। कृपा करौ जग जननि भवानी॥
केहि विधि स्तुति करौं तिहारी। सुधि लीजै अपराध बिसारी॥
कृपा दृष्टि चितवो मम ओरी। जगजननी विनती सुन मोरी॥

Jagjananī jai sindhu kumārī | Dīnan kī tum ho hitkārī ||
Vinvaun nitya tumahin mahārānī | Kripā karau jag janani Bhavānī ||
Kehi vidhi stuti karaun tihārī | Sudhi lījai aprādh bisārī ||
Kripā drishti chitvo mam orī | Jagjananī vintī sun morī ||

Victory to the Mother of the world, daughter of the ocean, friend of the poor. Every day I pray to you, great queen: be kind, Mother Bhavani. How can I praise you? Forget my faults and remember me. Turn your gracious eyes towards me, Mother, and hear my prayer.

Chaupai 9–12

ज्ञान बुद्धि जय सुख की दाता। संकट हरो हमारी माता॥
क्षीरसिन्धु जब विष्णु मथायो। चौदह रत्न सिन्धु में पायो॥
चौदह रत्न में तुम सुखरासी। सेवा कियो प्रभु बनि दासी॥
जब जब जन्म जहाँ प्रभु लीन्हा। रूप बदल तहँ सेवा कीन्हा॥

Gyān buddhi jai sukh kī dātā | Sankat haro hamārī mātā ||
Kshīrasindhu jab Vishnu mathāyo | Chaudah ratna sindhu mein pāyo ||
Chaudah ratna mein tum sukhrāsī | Sevā kiyo prabhu bani dāsī ||
Jab jab janma jahān prabhu līnhā | Rūp badal tahan sevā kīnhā ||

Giver of knowledge, wisdom and happiness, take away our troubles, Mother. When Vishnu had the ocean of milk churned, fourteen jewels came out of it; among them you were the treasure of joy, and you served the Lord as his own. Wherever the Lord took birth, you changed your form and served him there.

Chaupai 13–16

स्वयं विष्णु जब नर तनु धारा। लीन्हेउ अवधपुरी अवतारा॥
तब तुम प्रगट जनकपुर माहीं। सेवा कियो हृदय पुलकाहीं॥
अपनायो तोहि अन्तर्यामी। विश्व विदित त्रिभुवन की स्वामी॥
तुम सम प्रबल शक्ति नहिं आनी। कहँ लौं महिमा कहौं बखानी॥

Svayam Vishnu jab nar tanu dhārā | Līnheu Avadhpurī avtārā ||
Tab tum pragat Janakpur māhīn | Sevā kiyo hriday pulkāhīn ||
Apnāyo tohi antaryāmī | Vishva vidit tribhuvan kī swāmī ||
Tum sam prabal shakti nahin ānī | Kahan laun mahimā kahaun bakhānī ||

When Vishnu himself took a human body and was born in Ayodhya, you appeared in Janakpur and served him with a thrilled heart. The knower of hearts took you as his own, and you became the famed queen of the three worlds. There is no power as great as yours; how far can I tell your glory?

Chaupai 17–20

मन क्रम वचन करै सेवकाई। मन इच्छित वांछित फल पाई॥
तजि छल कपट और चतुराई। पूजहिं विविध भाँति मन लाई॥
और हाल मैं कहौं बुझाई। जो यह पाठ करै मन लाई॥
ताको कोई कष्ट न होई। मन इच्छित पावै फल सोई॥

Man kram vachan karai sevkāī | Man ichchhit vānchhit phal pāī ||
Taji chhal kapat aur chaturāī | Pūjahin vividh bhānti man lāī ||
Aur hāl main kahaun bujhāī | Jo yah pāth karai man lāī ||
Tāko koī kasht na hoī | Man ichchhit pāvai phal soī ||

Whoever serves you in thought, deed and word receives the fruit the heart desires. Leaving deceit and cleverness, they worship you in many ways with full attention. And I will tell you more: whoever recites this with the mind fixed on it suffers no hardship and gains what the heart wants.

Chaupai 21–24

त्राहि त्राहि जय दुःख निवारिणि। त्रिविध ताप भव बंधन हारिणि॥
जो चालीसा पढ़ै पढ़ावै। ध्यान लगाकर सुनै सुनावै॥
ताकौ कोई न रोग सतावै। पुत्र आदि धन सम्पत्ति पावै॥
पुत्रहीन अरु संपति हीना। अन्ध बधिर कोढ़ी अति दीना॥

Trāhi trāhi jai dukh nivārini | Trividh tāp bhav bandhan hārini ||
Jo chālīsā padhai padhāvai | Dhyān lagākar sunai sunāvai ||
Tākau koī na rog satāvai | Putra ādi dhan sampatti pāvai ||
Putrahīn aru sampati hīnā | Andh badhir kodhī ati dīnā ||

Save us, save us, remover of sorrow, who breaks the three kinds of suffering and the bonds of worldly life. Whoever reads this Chalisa or has it read, listens to it or recites it with attention, is troubled by no illness and gains children, wealth and property, even those without children or means, the blind, the deaf, the sick and the very poor.

Chaupai 25–28

विप्र बोलाय कै पाठ करावै। शंका दिल में कभी न लावै॥
पाठ करावै दिन चालीसा। ता पर कृपा करैं गौरीसा॥
सुख सम्पत्ति बहुत सी पावै। कमी नहीं काहू की आवै॥
बारह मास करै जो पूजा। तेहि सम धन्य और नहिं दूजा॥

Vipra bolāy kai pāth karāvai | Shankā dil mein kabhī na lāvai ||
Pāth karāvai din chālīsā | Tā par kripā karain gaurīsā ||
Sukh sampatti bahut sī pāvai | Kamī nahīn kāhū kī āvai ||
Bārah mās karai jo pūjā | Tehi sam dhanya aur nahin dūjā ||

If they call a priest to recite it, never letting doubt into their hearts, and have it read for forty days, the Lord of Gauri blesses them. They gain much happiness and wealth and lack nothing. No one is as blessed as the one who worships you all twelve months of the year.

Chaupai 29–32

प्रतिदिन पाठ करै मन माहीं। उन सम कोउ जग में कहुँ नाहीं॥
बहुविधि क्या मैं करौं बड़ाई। लेय परीक्षा ध्यान लगाई॥
करि विश्वास करै व्रत नेमा। होय सिद्ध उपजै उर प्रेमा॥
जय जय जय लक्ष्मी भवानी। सब में व्यापित हो गुण खानी॥

Pratidin pāth karai man māhīn | Un sam kou jag mein kahun nāhīn ||
Bahuvidhi kyā main karaun badāī | Ley parīkshā dhyān lagāī ||
Kari vishvās karai vrat nemā | Hoy siddh upjai ur premā ||
Jai jai jai Lakshmī Bhavānī | Sab mein vyāpit ho gun khānī ||

There is no one in the world like the one who recites it in their heart every day. Why should I praise it in so many ways? Try it with attention and see. Whoever keeps the fast and its rules with faith succeeds, and love wells up in the heart. Victory to Lakshmi Bhavani, mine of virtue, present in all.

Chaupai 33–36

तुम्हरो तेज प्रबल जग माहीं। तुम सम कोउ दयालु कहुँ नाहीं॥
मोहि अनाथ की सुधि अब लीजै। संकट काटि भक्ति मोहि दीजै॥
भूल चूक करि क्षमा हमारी। दर्शन दीजै दशा निहारी॥
बिन दर्शन व्याकुल अधिकारी। तुमहि अछत दुःख सहते भारी॥

Tumharo tej prabal jag māhīn | Tum sam kou dayālu kahun nāhīn ||
Mohi anāth kī sudhi ab lījai | Sankat kāti bhakti mohi dījai ||
Bhūl chūk kari kshamā hamārī | Darshan dījai dashā nihārī ||
Bin darshan vyākul adhikārī | Tumahi achhat dukh sahte bhārī ||

Your radiance is mighty in the world, and no one is as kind as you. Remember me now, helpless as I am: cut away my troubles and give me devotion. Forgive our mistakes and, seeing our state, show yourself to us. Without the sight of you your devotee is restless and suffers greatly, though you are here.

Chaupai 37–40

नहिं मोहिं ज्ञान बुद्धि है तन में। सब जानत हो अपने मन में॥
रूप चतुर्भुज करके धारण। कष्ट मोर अब करहु निवारण॥
केहि प्रकार मैं करौं बड़ाई। ज्ञान बुद्धि मोहि नहिं अधिकाई॥

Nahin mohin gyān buddhi hai tan mein | Sab jānat ho apne man mein ||
Rūp chaturbhuj karke dhāran | Kasht mor ab karahu nivāran ||
Kehi prakār main karaun badāī | Gyān buddhi mohi nahin adhikāī ||

I have no knowledge or wisdom of my own; you know everything in your heart. Take your four-armed form and remove my troubles now. How can I praise you, when I have so little knowledge or understanding?

Doha

त्राहि त्राहि दुख हारिणी, हरो वेगि सब त्रास।
जयति जयति जय लक्ष्मी, करो शत्रु को नाश॥
रामदास धरि ध्यान नित, विनय करत कर जोर।
मातु लक्ष्मी दास पर, करहु दया की कोर॥

Trāhi trāhi dukh hārinī, haro vegi sab trās |
Jayti jayti jai Lakshmī, karo shatru ko nāsh ||
Rāmdās dhari dhyān nit, vinay karat kar jor |
Mātu Lakshmī dās par, karahu dayā kī kor ||

Save us, remover of sorrow, and quickly take away all fear; victory, victory to Lakshmi, destroy what stands against us. Ramdas meditates on you daily and prays with folded hands: Mother Lakshmi, cast a glance of mercy on your servant.
