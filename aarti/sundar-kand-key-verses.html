Sundar Kand: Key Verses

सुन्दरकाण्ड (मुख्य अंश)

HanumanDeity
StutiForm
Tuesday, SaturdayDay
Hanuman Jayanti, TuesdayOccasion

Sundar Kand is the fifth book of Tulsidas's Ramcharitmanas, telling of Hanuman's journey to Lanka to find Sita. The full text (about 60 dohas) is often recited on Tuesdays and Saturdays; these are its opening invocations and best-loved verses. For the complete text, use the Gita Press edition of the Ramcharitmanas.

Shloka 1 (Sanskrit)

शान्तं शाश्वतमप्रमेयमनघं निर्वाणशान्तिप्रदं
ब्रह्माशम्भुफणीन्द्रसेव्यमनिशं वेदान्तवेद्यं विभुम्।
रामाख्यं जगदीश्वरं सुरगुरुं मायामनुष्यं हरिं
वन्देऽहं करुणाकरं रघुवरं भूपालचूडामणिम्॥

Shāntam shāshvatamaprameyamanagham nirvānashāntipradam
Brahmāshambhuphanīndrasevyamanisham vedāntavedyam vibhum |
Rāmākhyam jagadīshvaram suragurum māyāmanushyam harim
Vande'ham karunākaram raghuvaram bhūpālachūdāmanim ||

I bow to Rama, Lord of the universe: peaceful, eternal, beyond measure, sinless, giver of the peace of liberation; ever served by Brahma, Shiva and Shesha, known through Vedanta, all-pervading; the teacher of the gods, Hari appearing in human form by his own power, ocean of compassion, best of the Raghus and jewel among kings.

Shloka 2 (Sanskrit)

नान्या स्पृहा रघुपते हृदयेऽस्मदीये
सत्यं वदामि च भवानखिलान्तरात्मा।
भक्तिं प्रयच्छ रघुपुङ्गव निर्भरां मे
कामादिदोषरहितं कुरु मानसं च॥

Nānyā sprihā raghupate hridaye'smadīye
Satyam vadāmi cha bhavānakhilāntarātmā |
Bhaktim prayachchha raghupungava nirbharām me
Kāmādidosharahitam kuru mānasam cha ||

Lord of the Raghus, I speak the truth: there is no other longing in my heart, and you, the inner Self of all, know it. Best of the Raghus, grant me complete devotion and free my mind from desire and every other fault.

Shloka 3 (Sanskrit)

अतुलितबलधामं हेमशैलाभदेहं
दनुजवनकृशानुं ज्ञानिनामग्रगण्यम्।
सकलगुणनिधानं वानराणामधीशं
रघुपतिप्रियभक्तं वातजातं नमामि॥

Atulitabaladhāmam hemashailābhadeham
Danujavanakrishānum gyānināmagraganyam |
Sakalagunanidhānam vānarānāmadhīsham
Raghupatipriyabhaktam vātajātam namāmi ||

I bow to the son of the Wind: home of matchless strength, his body shining like a golden mountain, a fire to the forest of demons, foremost among the wise, treasury of all virtues, lord of the vanaras and dear devotee of Rama.

Chaupai

जामवंत के बचन सुहाए। सुनि हनुमंत हृदय अति भाए॥
तब लगि मोहि परिखेहु तुम्ह भाई। सहि दुख कंद मूल फल खाई॥
जब लगि आवौं सीतहि देखी। होइहि काजु मोहि हरष बिसेषी॥

Jāmvant ke bachan suhāe | Suni Hanumant hriday ati bhāe ||
Tab lagi mohi parikhehu tumh bhāī | Sahi dukh kand mūl phal khāī ||
Jab lagi āvaun sītahi dekhī | Hoihi kāju mohi harash biseshī ||

Jambavan's encouraging words delighted Hanuman's heart. 'Brothers, wait for me here, enduring hardship and living on roots and fruit, until I return having seen Sita. The work will be done; I feel great joy.'

Chaupai

प्रबिसि नगर कीजे सब काजा। हृदयँ राखि कोसलपुर राजा॥

Prabisi nagar kīje sab kājā | Hridayan rākhi kosalpur rājā ||

Enter the city and accomplish every task, keeping the King of Ayodhya in your heart. (Often recited before beginning any undertaking.)

Doha 1

हनूमान तेहि परसा कर पुनि कीन्ह प्रनाम।
राम काजु कीन्हें बिनु मोहि कहाँ बिश्राम॥

Hanūmān tehi parsā kar puni kīnh pranām |
Rām kāju kīnhen binu mohi kahān bishrām ||

Hanuman touched the mountain Mainaka with his hand, bowed and said: 'How can I rest until Rama's work is done?'

Chaupai

तब देखी मुद्रिका मनोहर। राम नाम अंकित अति सुंदर॥
चकित चितव मुदरी पहिचानी। हरष बिषाद हृदयँ अकुलानी॥

Tab dekhī mudrikā manohar | Rām nām ankit ati sundar ||
Chakit chitav mudrī pahichānī | Harash bishād hridayan akulānī ||

Then Sita saw the lovely ring, engraved with Rama's name. Amazed, she recognised it, and her heart was stirred with both joy and grief.

Chaupai

अजर अमर गुननिधि सुत होहू। करहुँ बहुत रघुनायक छोहू॥

Ajar amar gunanidhi sut hohū | Karhun bahut raghunāyak chhohū ||

(Sita blesses Hanuman:) 'My son, may you be ever young, immortal and a treasury of virtues, and may Rama always shower his love on you.'

Closing Doha

सकल सुमंगल दायक रघुनायक गुन गान।
सादर सुनहिं ते तरहिं भव सिंधु बिना जलजान॥

Sakal sumangal dāyak raghunāyak gun gān |
Sādar sunahin te tarahin bhav sindhu binā jaljān ||

Singing the virtues of Rama gives every blessing; those who listen with reverence cross the ocean of worldly life without needing a boat.
