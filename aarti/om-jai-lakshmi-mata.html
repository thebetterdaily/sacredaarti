04 · AARTI

Om Jai Lakshmi Mata

ॐ जय लक्ष्मी माता

LakshmiDeity
AartiForm
FridayDay
Diwali, DhanterasOccasion

Sung on Diwali night after the Lakshmi-Ganesh puja and every Friday. It praises Lakshmi as the source of all prosperity and as one with Uma (Parvati) and Brahmani (Saraswati).

ॐ जय लक्ष्मी माता, मैया जय लक्ष्मी माता।
तुमको निशिदिन सेवत, हर विष्णु विधाता॥

Om jai Lakshmī mātā, maiyā jai Lakshmī mātā |
Tumko nishidin sevat, har Vishnu vidhātā ||

Glory to Mother Lakshmi, whom Shiva, Vishnu and Brahma serve day and night.

उमा रमा ब्रह्माणी, तुम ही जग माता।
सूर्य चन्द्रमा ध्यावत, नारद ऋषि गाता॥

Umā ramā brahmānī, tum hī jag mātā |
Sūrya chandramā dhyāvat, Nārad rishi gātā ||

You are Uma, Rama and Brahmani, the mother of the world; the sun and moon meditate on you and the sage Narada sings your praise.

॥ ॐ जय लक्ष्मी माता ॥Om jai Lakshmī mātā

दुर्गा रूप निरंजनी, सुख सम्पत्ति दाता।
जो कोई तुमको ध्यावत, ऋद्धि सिद्धि धन पाता॥

Durgā rūp niranjanī, sukh sampatti dātā |
Jo koī tumko dhyāvat, riddhi siddhi dhan pātā ||

In your pure form as Durga you give happiness and wealth; whoever meditates on you gains prosperity, success and riches.

॥ ॐ जय लक्ष्मी माता ॥Om jai Lakshmī mātā

तुम पाताल निवासिनी, तुम ही शुभदाता।
कर्म प्रभाव प्रकाशिनी, भवनिधि की त्राता॥

Tum pātāl nivāsinī, tum hī shubhdātā |
Karm prabhāv prakāshinī, bhavnidhi kī trātā ||

You dwell even in the netherworld and you alone bestow what is auspicious; you reveal the fruit of our deeds and carry us across the ocean of worldly life.

॥ ॐ जय लक्ष्मी माता ॥Om jai Lakshmī mātā

जिस घर में तुम रहतीं, सब सद्गुण आता।
सब सम्भव हो जाता, मन नहीं घबराता॥

Jis ghar mein tum rahtīn, sab sadgun ātā |
Sab sambhav ho jātā, man nahīn ghabrātā ||

Every virtue comes to the home where you live; everything becomes possible there and the mind is free of fear.

॥ ॐ जय लक्ष्मी माता ॥Om jai Lakshmī mātā

तुम बिन यज्ञ न होते, वस्त्र न कोई पाता।
खान पान का वैभव, सब तुमसे आता॥

Tum bin yagya na hote, vastra na koī pātā |
Khān pān kā vaibhav, sab tumse ātā ||

Without you no sacrifice can be performed and no one has clothing; the richness of food and drink all comes from you.

॥ ॐ जय लक्ष्मी माता ॥Om jai Lakshmī mātā

शुभ गुण मन्दिर सुन्दर, क्षीरोदधि जाता।
रत्न चतुर्दश तुम बिन, कोई नहीं पाता॥

Shubh gun mandir sundar, kshīrodadhi jātā |
Ratna chaturdash tum bin, koī nahīn pātā ||

Beautiful temple of every good quality, born of the ocean of milk: without you no one could obtain the fourteen jewels.

॥ ॐ जय लक्ष्मी माता ॥Om jai Lakshmī mātā

महालक्ष्मीजी की आरती, जो कोई जन गाता।
उर आनन्द समाता, पाप उतर जाता॥

Mahālakshmījī kī ārtī, jo koī jan gātā |
Ur ānand samātā, pāp utar jātā ||

Whoever sings the aarti of Mahalakshmi is filled with joy and freed from sin.

॥ ॐ जय लक्ष्मी माता ॥Om jai Lakshmī mātā

The refrain ॥ ॐ जय लक्ष्मी माता ॥ is sung after every stanza, as marked.
