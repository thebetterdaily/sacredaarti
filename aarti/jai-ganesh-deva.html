01 · AARTI

Jai Ganesh Deva

जय गणेश जय गणेश देवा

GaneshaDeity
AartiForm
WednesdayDay
Ganesh Chaturthi, DiwaliOccasion

Sung first in almost every Hindu household puja, because Ganesha is worshipped before any other deity. The line naming 'Soor Shyam' carries the signature of the poet Surdas, to whom the aarti is traditionally attributed.

जय गणेश, जय गणेश, जय गणेश देवा।
माता जाकी पार्वती, पिता महादेवा॥

Jai Ganesh, jai Ganesh, jai Ganesh devā |
Mātā jākī Pārvatī, pitā Mahādevā ||

Victory to Lord Ganesha, whose mother is Parvati and whose father is Mahadeva (Shiva).

एकदन्त दयावन्त, चार भुजा धारी।
माथे सिन्दूर सोहे, मूसे की सवारी॥

Ekdant dayāvant, chār bhujā dhārī |
Māthe sindūr sohe, mūse kī savārī ||

The single-tusked, compassionate Lord has four arms; vermilion shines on his forehead and he rides a mouse.

॥ जय गणेश, जय गणेश, जय गणेश देवा ॥Jai Ganesh, jai Ganesh, jai Ganesh devā

पान चढ़े, फूल चढ़े, और चढ़े मेवा।
लड्डुअन का भोग लगे, सन्त करें सेवा॥

Pān chadhe, phūl chadhe, aur chadhe mevā |
Laddu-an kā bhog lage, sant karen sevā ||

Betel leaves, flowers and dry fruits are offered to him; laddoos are his favourite food, and the saints serve him.

॥ जय गणेश, जय गणेश, जय गणेश देवा ॥Jai Ganesh, jai Ganesh, jai Ganesh devā

अन्धन को आँख देत, कोढ़िन को काया।
बाँझन को पुत्र देत, निर्धन को माया॥

Andhan ko ānkh det, kodhin ko kāyā |
Bānjhan ko putra det, nirdhan ko māyā ||

He gives sight to the blind, a healthy body to the afflicted, children to the childless and wealth to the poor.

॥ जय गणेश, जय गणेश, जय गणेश देवा ॥Jai Ganesh, jai Ganesh, jai Ganesh devā

'सूर' श्याम शरण आए, सफल कीजे सेवा।
माता जाकी पार्वती, पिता महादेवा॥

'Sūr' shyām sharan āe, saphal kīje sevā |
Mātā jākī Pārvatī, pitā Mahādevā ||

Surdas has come to your shelter, O Lord: make his service fruitful.

॥ जय गणेश, जय गणेश, जय गणेश देवा ॥Jai Ganesh, jai Ganesh, jai Ganesh devā

दीनन की लाज रखो, शम्भु सुतकारी।
कामना को पूर्ण करो, जाऊँ बलिहारी॥

Dīnan kī lāj rakho, Shambhu sutkārī |
Kāmnā ko pūrna karo, jāūn balihārī ||

O son of Shambhu, protect the honour of the humble and fulfil our wishes; I offer myself to you.

॥ जय गणेश, जय गणेश, जय गणेश देवा ॥Jai Ganesh, jai Ganesh, jai Ganesh devā

The refrain ॥ जय गणेश, जय गणेश, जय गणेश देवा ॥ is sung after every stanza, as marked.
