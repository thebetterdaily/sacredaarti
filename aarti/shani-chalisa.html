26 · CHALISA

Shri Shani Chalisa

श्री शनि चालीसा

ShaniDeity
ChalisaForm
SaturdayDay
Shani Jayanti, Shani AmavasyaOccasion

Recited on Saturdays to Shani Dev, the planet of karma and justice. It tells how Shani's influence touched even Rama, King Vikramaditya, Harishchandra, Nala and the Pandavas, and closes with the signature of the poet Ram Sundar.

Doha

जय गणेश गिरिजा सुवन, मंगल करण कृपाल।
दीनन के दुख दूर करि, कीजै नाथ निहाल॥
जय जय श्री शनिदेव प्रभु, सुनहु विनय महाराज।
करहु कृपा हे रवि तनय, राखहु जन की लाज॥

Jai Ganesh Girijā suvan, mangal karan kripāl |
Dīnan ke dukh dūr kari, kījai nāth nihāl ||
Jai jai shrī Shanidev prabhu, sunahu vinay mahārāj |
Karahu kripā he Ravi tanay, rākhahu jan kī lāj ||

Glory to Ganesha, son of Girija, kind bringer of blessings: remove the sorrows of the humble and make them happy. Glory to Shri Shanidev: great king, hear my prayer. Son of the Sun, be gracious and protect your devotee's honour.

Chaupai 1–4

जयति जयति शनिदेव दयाला। करत सदा भक्तन प्रतिपाला॥
चारि भुजा तनु श्याम विराजै। माथे रतन मुकुट छबि छाजै॥
परम विशाल मनोहर भाला। टेढ़ी दृष्टि भृकुटि विकराला॥
कुण्डल श्रवण चमाचम चमके। हिय माल मुक्तन मणि दमके॥

Jayti jayti Shanidev dayālā | Karat sadā bhaktan pratipālā ||
Chāri bhujā tanu shyām virājai | Māthe ratan mukut chhabi chhājai ||
Param vishāl manohar bhālā | Tedhī drishti bhrikuti vikrālā ||
Kundal shravan chamācham chamke | Hiy māl muktan mani damke ||

Victory to kind Shanidev, who always protects his devotees. Four-armed and dark-bodied, a jewelled crown adorns his head. His forehead is broad and handsome, his gaze sidelong and his brows fierce. Earrings sparkle at his ears and a garland of pearls and gems glitters on his chest.

Chaupai 5–8

कर में गदा त्रिशूल कुठारा। पल बिच करैं अरिहिं संहारा॥
पिंगल कृष्णो छाया नन्दन। यम कोणस्थ रौद्र दुख भंजन॥
सौरी मन्द शनी दश नामा। भानु पुत्र पूजहिं सब कामा॥
जा पर प्रभु प्रसन्न ह्वैं जाहीं। रंकहुँ राव करैं क्षण माहीं॥

Kar mein gadā trishūl kuthārā | Pal bich karain arihin sanhārā ||
Pingal krishno chhāyā nandan | Yam konasth raudra dukh bhanjan ||
Saurī mand shanī dash nāmā | Bhānu putra pūjahin sab kāmā ||
Jā par prabhu prasanna hvain jāhīn | Rankahun rāv karain kshan māhīn ||

With mace, trident and axe in hand, he destroys enemies in a moment. Pingala, Krishna, Chhaya-nandan, Yama, Konastha, Raudra, Dukhabhanjana, Sauri, Manda and Shani are his ten names; worshipping the Sun's son fulfils every wish. When the Lord is pleased with someone, he turns a pauper into a king in an instant.

Chaupai 9–12

पर्वतहू तृण होई निहारत। तृणहू को पर्वत करि डारत॥
राज मिलत बन रामहिं दीन्हो। कैकेइहुँ की मति हरि लीन्हो॥
बनहूँ में मृग कपट दिखाई। मातु जानकी गई चुराई॥
लखनहिं शक्ति विकल करि डारा। मचिगा दल में हाहाकारा॥

Parvatahū trin hoī nihārat | Trinhū ko parvat kari dārat ||
Rāj milat ban rāmahin dīnho | Kaikeihun kī mati Hari līnho ||
Banhūn mein mrig kapat dikhāī | Mātu Jānkī gaī churāī ||
Lakhnahin shakti vikal kari dārā | Machigā dal mein hāhākārā ||

At his glance a mountain becomes a blade of grass, and a blade of grass becomes a mountain. Just as Rama was to be crowned, he was sent to the forest, for Kaikeyi's mind was clouded. In the forest the false deer appeared and Mother Janaki was abducted. Lakshmana was struck down by the Shakti weapon and the army cried out in despair.

Chaupai 13–16

रावण की गति मति बौराई। रामचन्द्र सों बैर बढ़ाई॥
दियो कीट करि कंचन लंका। बजि बजरंग बीर की डंका॥
नृप विक्रम पर तुहि पगु धारा। चित्र मयूर निगलि गै हारा॥
हार नौलखा लाग्यो चोरी। हाथ पैर डरवायो तोरी॥

Rāvan kī gati mati baurāī | Rāmchandra son bair badhāī ||
Diyo kīt kari kanchan Lankā | Baji bajrang bīr kī dankā ||
Nrip vikram par tuhi pagu dhārā | Chitra mayūr nigli gai hārā ||
Hār naulkhā lāgyo chorī | Hāth pair darvāyo torī ||

Ravana's mind went astray and he made an enemy of Rama; golden Lanka was reduced to dust, and brave Hanuman's drum resounded. When you set foot on King Vikramaditya, a painted peacock swallowed a necklace; he was accused of stealing the precious necklace and his hands and feet were broken.

Chaupai 17–20

भारी दशा निकृष्ट दिखायो। तेलिहिं घर कोल्हू चलवायो॥
विनय राग दीपक महं कीन्हयों। तब प्रसन्न प्रभु ह्वै सुख दीन्हयों॥
हरिश्चन्द्र नृप नारि बिकानी। आपहुं भरे डोम घर पानी॥
तैसे नल पर दशा सिरानी। भूंजी मीन कूद गई पानी॥

Bhārī dashā nikrisht dikhāyo | Telihin ghar kolhū chalvāyo ||
Vinay rāg dīpak maham kīnhayon | Tab prasanna prabhu hvai sukh dīnhayon ||
Harishchandra nrip nāri bikānī | Āphun bhare dom ghar pānī ||
Taise nal par dashā sirānī | Bhūnjī mīn kūd gaī pānī ||

In his harsh period the king was made to turn an oil press in an oil-seller's house; when he prayed to you in Raga Deepak, you were pleased and restored his happiness. King Harishchandra's wife was sold, and he himself carried water in a cremation-keeper's house. Likewise misfortune fell on King Nala, when even a roasted fish leapt back into the water.

Chaupai 21–24

श्री शंकरहिं गह्यो जब जाई। पारवती को सती कराई॥
तनिक विलोकत ही करि रीसा। नभ उड़ि गयो गौरिसुत सीसा॥
पाण्डव पर भै दशा तुम्हारी। बची द्रौपदी होति उघारी॥
कौरव के भी गति मति मारयो। युद्ध महाभारत करि डारयो॥

Shrī shankarahin gahyo jab jāī | Pāravtī ko satī karāī ||
Tanik vilokat hī kari rīsā | Nabh udi gayo Gaurisut sīsā ||
Pāndav par bhai dashā tumhārī | Bachī Draupadī hoti ughārī ||
Kaurav ke bhī gati mati māryo | Yuddh mahābhārat kari dāryo ||

When your influence reached even Lord Shankar, Parvati had to become Sati in the fire. At a single displeased glance from you, the head of Gauri's son flew off into the sky. When your period fell on the Pandavas, Draupadi was barely saved from being disrobed. You clouded the minds of the Kauravas too, and brought about the Mahabharata war.

Chaupai 25–28

रवि कहँ मुख महँ धरि तत्काला। लेकर कूदि परयो पाताला॥
शेष देव लखि विनती लाई। रवि को मुख ते दियो छुड़ाई॥
वाहन प्रभु के सात सुजाना। जग दिग्गज गर्दभ मृग स्वाना॥
जम्बुक सिंह आदि नख धारी। सो फल ज्योतिष कहत पुकारी॥

Ravi kahan mukh mahan dhari tatkālā | Lekar kūdi paryo pātālā ||
Shesh dev lakhi vintī lāī | Ravi ko mukh te diyo chhudāī ||
Vāhan prabhu ke sāt sujānā | Jag diggaj gardabh mrig svānā ||
Jambuk sinh ādi nakh dhārī | So phal jyotish kahat pukārī ||

You seized the Sun in your mouth and leapt into the netherworld; only when the gods pleaded did you release him. Know that the Lord has seven mounts: the elephant, donkey, deer, dog, jackal, lion and other clawed beasts; astrologers declare the result of each.

Chaupai 29–32

गज वाहन लक्ष्मी गृह आवैं। हय ते सुख सम्पत्ति उपजावैं॥
गर्दभ हानि करै बहु काजा। सिंह सिद्धकर राज समाजा॥
जम्बुक बुद्धि नष्ट कर डारै। मृग दे कष्ट प्राण संहारै॥
जब आवहिं प्रभु स्वान सवारी। चोरी आदि होय डर भारी॥

Gaj vāhan Lakshmī grih āvain | Hay te sukh sampatti upjāvain ||
Gardabh hāni karai bahu kājā | Sinh siddhakar rāj samājā ||
Jambuk buddhi nasht kar dārai | Mrig de kasht prān sanhārai ||
Jab āvahin prabhu svān savārī | Chorī ādi hoy dar bhārī ||

When he comes on the elephant, Lakshmi enters the home; on the horse he brings happiness and wealth. The donkey brings losses in many tasks; the lion brings success and standing in society. The jackal destroys good judgement; the deer brings hardship and danger to life. When the Lord comes riding a dog, there is fear of theft and similar troubles.

Chaupai 33–36

तैसहि चारि चरण यह नामा। स्वर्ण लौह चाँदी अरु तामा॥
लौह चरण पर जब प्रभु आवैं। धन जन सम्पत्ति नष्ट करावैं॥
समता ताम्र रजत शुभकारी। स्वर्ण सर्व सुख मंगल भारी॥
जो यह शनि चरित्र नित गावै। कबहुं न दशा निकृष्ट सतावै॥

Taisahi chāri charan yah nāmā | Svarna lauh chāndī aru tāmā ||
Lauh charan par jab prabhu āvain | Dhan jan sampatti nasht karāvain ||
Samtā tāmra rajat shubhkārī | Svarna sarva sukh mangal bhārī ||
Jo yah Shani charitra nit gāvai | Kabhun na dashā nikrisht satāvai ||

Likewise his four 'feet' are named gold, iron, silver and copper. When the Lord comes on the iron foot, wealth, people and property are lost. Copper brings balance and silver is auspicious; gold brings every happiness and great blessings. Whoever sings this story of Shani daily is never troubled by a bad period.

Chaupai 37–40

अद्भुत नाथ दिखावैं लीला। करैं शत्रु के नशि बलि ढीला॥
जो पण्डित सुयोग्य बुलवाई। विधिवत शनि ग्रह शांति कराई॥
पीपल जल शनि दिवस चढ़ावत। दीप दान दै बहु सुख पावत॥
कहत राम सुन्दर प्रभु दासा। शनि सुमिरत सुख होत प्रकाशा॥

Adbhut nāth dikhāvain līlā | Karain shatru ke nashi bali dhīlā ||
Jo pandit suyogya bulvāī | Vidhivat Shani grah shānti karāī ||
Pīpal jal Shani divas chadhāvat | Dīp dān dai bahu sukh pāvat ||
Kahat Rām sundar prabhu dāsā | Shani sumirat sukh hot prakāshā ||

The Lord shows wondrous play, weakening and destroying the enemy's strength. Whoever invites a qualified priest to perform the Shani graha-shanti ritual properly, or offers water to the peepal tree on Saturday and donates a lamp, gains much happiness. Ram Sundar, servant of the Lord, says: remembering Shani brings the light of happiness.

Doha

पाठ शनिश्चर देव को, की हों 'भक्त' तैयार।
करत पाठ चालीस दिन, हो भवसागर पार॥

Pāth shanishchar dev ko, kī hon 'bhakt' taiyār |
Karat pāth chālīs din, ho bhavsāgar pār ||

The devotee who prepares and recites this prayer to Shanishchar Dev for forty days crosses the ocean of worldly life.
