24 · AARTI

Om Jai Brihaspati Deva

ॐ जय बृहस्पति देवा

Brihaspati DevDeity
AartiForm
ThursdayDay
Brihaspativar Vrat (Thursdays)Occasion

The aarti of Brihaspati Dev, the guru of the gods and the lord of Thursday (Guruvar). It closes the Thursday fast, when yellow clothes are worn and bananas, gram dal and yellow sweets are offered. The last line carries the poet's name, Jethanand.

ॐ जय बृहस्पति देवा, स्वामी जय बृहस्पति देवा।
छिन छिन भोग लगाऊँ, कदली फल मेवा॥

Om jai Brihaspati devā, swāmī jai Brihaspati devā |
Chhin chhin bhog lagāūn, kadlī phal mevā ||

Victory to Lord Brihaspati, O Master; every moment I offer you bananas and dry fruits.

तुम पूरण परमात्मा, तुम अन्तर्यामी।
जगतपिता जगदीश्वर, तुम सबके स्वामी॥

Tum pūran parmātmā, tum antaryāmī |
Jagatpitā jagdīshvar, tum sabke swāmī ||

You are the complete Supreme Self who knows every heart; father and lord of the world, you are master of all.

॥ ॐ जय बृहस्पति देवा, स्वामी जय बृहस्पति देवा ॥Om jai Brihaspati devā, swāmī jai Brihaspati devā

चरणामृत निज निर्मल, सब पातक हर्ता।
सकल मनोरथ दायक, कृपा करो भर्ता॥

Charnāmrit nij nirmal, sab pātak hartā |
Sakal manorath dāyak, kripā karo bhartā ||

The pure water that has washed your feet takes away every sin; O sustainer, giver of all wishes, be gracious to us.

॥ ॐ जय बृहस्पति देवा, स्वामी जय बृहस्पति देवा ॥Om jai Brihaspati devā, swāmī jai Brihaspati devā

तन, मन, धन अर्पण कर, जो जन शरण पड़े।
प्रभु प्रकट तब होकर, आकर द्वार खड़े॥

Tan, man, dhan arpan kar, jo jan sharan pade |
Prabhu prakat tab hokar, ākar dvār khade ||

When someone offers body, mind and wealth and takes refuge in you, the Lord appears and comes to stand at their door.

॥ ॐ जय बृहस्पति देवा, स्वामी जय बृहस्पति देवा ॥Om jai Brihaspati devā, swāmī jai Brihaspati devā

दीनदयाल दयानिधि, भक्तन हितकारी।
पाप दोष सब हर्ता, भव बंधन हारी॥

Dīndayāl dayānidhi, bhaktan hitkārī |
Pāp dosh sab hartā, bhav bandhan hārī ||

Kind to the humble and an ocean of mercy, you are the well-wisher of devotees; you remove all sins and faults and free us from worldly bondage.

॥ ॐ जय बृहस्पति देवा, स्वामी जय बृहस्पति देवा ॥Om jai Brihaspati devā, swāmī jai Brihaspati devā

सकल मनोरथ दायक, सब संशय हारो।
विषय विकार मिटाओ, संतन सुखकारी॥

Sakal manorath dāyak, sab sanshay hāro |
Vishay vikār mitāo, santan sukhkārī ||

Giver of every wish, take away all our doubts; wipe out the disorders of the senses, O bringer of joy to the saints.

॥ ॐ जय बृहस्पति देवा, स्वामी जय बृहस्पति देवा ॥Om jai Brihaspati devā, swāmī jai Brihaspati devā

जो कोई आरती तेरी, प्रेम सहित गावे।
जेठानन्द आनन्दकर, सो निश्चय पावे॥

Jo koī ārtī terī, prem sahit gāve |
Jethānand ānandakar, so nishchay pāve ||

Whoever sings your aarti with love, says Jethanand, will surely attain joy.

॥ ॐ जय बृहस्पति देवा, स्वामी जय बृहस्पति देवा ॥Om jai Brihaspati devā, swāmī jai Brihaspati devā

The refrain ॥ ॐ जय बृहस्पति देवा, स्वामी जय बृहस्पति देवा ॥ is sung after every stanza, as marked.
