Sacred Aarti / Shivji / Mantra

37 · MANTRA

Shiv Panchakshar Stotram (Om Namah Shivaya)

शिव पञ्चाक्षर स्तोत्रम्

ShivjiDeity
MantraForm
MondayDay
Mahashivratri, Shravan MondaysOccasion

A Sanskrit hymn attributed to Adi Shankaracharya, built on the five syllables of the mantra 'Na-ma-shi-vā-ya'. Each verse praises Shivji and ends on one of the five syllables, so reciting it is a way of chanting Om Namah Shivaya with its meaning.

नागेन्द्रहाराय त्रिलोचनाय भस्माङ्गरागाय महेश्वराय।
नित्याय शुद्धाय दिगम्बराय तस्मै नकाराय नमः शिवाय॥

Nāgendrahārāya trilochanāya bhasmāngarāgāya maheshvarāya |
Nityāya shuddhāya digambarāya tasmai nakārāya namah shivāya ||

Salutations to Shivji, the syllable 'Na': who wears the king of serpents as a garland, three-eyed, his body smeared with ash; the great lord, eternal, pure, clothed only by the directions.

मन्दाकिनीसलिलचन्दनचर्चिताय नन्दीश्वरप्रमथनाथमहेश्वराय।
मन्दारपुष्पबहुपुष्पसुपूजिताय तस्मै मकाराय नमः शिवाय॥

Mandākinīsalilachandanacharchitāya nandīshvarapramathanāthamaheshvarāya |
Mandārapushpabahupushpasupūjitāya tasmai makārāya namah shivāya ||

Salutations to Shivji, the syllable 'Ma': anointed with the water of Ganga Maiya and with sandal paste, lord of Nandi and of the ganas, worshipped with mandara and many other flowers.

शिवाय गौरीवदनाब्जवृन्दसूर्याय दक्षाध्वरनाशकाय।
श्रीनीलकण्ठाय वृषध्वजाय तस्मै शिकाराय नमः शिवाय॥

Shivāya gaurīvadanābjavrindasūryāya dakshādhvaranāshakāya |
Shrīnīlakanthāya vrishadhvajāya tasmai shikārāya namah shivāya ||

Salutations to Shivji, the syllable 'Shi': the auspicious one, the sun that opens the lotus of Gauri's face, destroyer of Daksha's sacrifice, the blue-throated one whose banner bears the bull.

वसिष्ठकुम्भोद्भवगौतमार्यमुनीन्द्रदेवार्चितशेखराय।
चन्द्रार्कवैश्वानरलोचनाय तस्मै वकाराय नमः शिवाय॥

Vasishthakumbhodbhavagautamāryamunīndradevārchitashekharāya |
Chandrārkavaishvānaralochanāya tasmai vakārāya namah shivāya ||

Salutations to Shivji, the syllable 'Va': whose head is worshipped by Vasishtha, Agastya, Gautama and the great sages and gods, and whose three eyes are the moon, the sun and fire.

यक्षस्वरूपाय जटाधराय पिनाकहस्ताय सनातनाय।
दिव्याय देवाय दिगम्बराय तस्मै यकाराय नमः शिवाय॥

Yakshasvarūpāya jatādharāya pinākahastāya sanātanāya |
Divyāya devāya digambarāya tasmai yakārāya namah shivāya ||

Salutations to Shivji, the syllable 'Ya': who took the form of a yaksha, who wears matted hair and holds the Pinaka bow; the eternal, the divine, the god clothed by the directions.

Phalashruti

पञ्चाक्षरमिदं पुण्यं यः पठेच्छिवसन्निधौ।
शिवलोकमवाप्नोति शिवेन सह मोदते॥

Panchāksharamidam punyam yah pathechchhivasannidhau |
Shivalokamavāpnoti shivena saha modate ||

Whoever recites this holy hymn of five syllables in the presence of Shivji attains Shivji's realm and rejoices with Shivji.
