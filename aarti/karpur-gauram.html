Sacred Aarti / Shiva

16 · MANTRA

Karpur Gauram

कर्पूरगौरं करुणावतारं

ShivaDeity
MantraForm
Daily, MondayDay
After every aartiOccasion

Traditionally chanted immediately after any aarti, while the lamp is still lit, whichever deity has been worshipped. It praises Shiva together with Bhavani (Parvati).

कर्पूरगौरं करुणावतारं संसारसारं भुजगेन्द्रहारम्।
सदा वसन्तं हृदयारविन्दे भवं भवानीसहितं नमामि॥

Karpūragauram karunāvatāram sansārasāram bhujagendrahāram |
Sadā vasantam hridayāravinde bhavam bhavānīsahitam namāmi ||

I bow to Shiva, together with Bhavani: white as camphor, compassion incarnate, the essence of the world, wearing the king of serpents as a garland, who dwells for ever in the lotus of the heart.
