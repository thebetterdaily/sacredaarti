Sacred Aarti / Santoshi Mata / Aarti

20 · AARTI

Jai Santoshi Mata

जय सन्तोषी माता

Santoshi MataDeity
AartiForm
FridayDay
Santoshi Mata Vrat (Fridays)Occasion

The aarti of Santoshi Mata, the goddess of contentment, sung at the end of the Friday fast kept in her honour. Jaggery and roasted chana are her offering, and sour food is avoided on the day of the fast.

जय सन्तोषी माता, मैया जय सन्तोषी माता।
अपने सेवक जन को, सुख सम्पति दाता॥

Jai Santoshī mātā, maiyā jai Santoshī mātā |
Apne sevak jan ko, sukh sampati dātā ||

Victory to Santoshi Mata, O Mother, who gives happiness and prosperity to those who serve her.

सुन्दर चीर सुनहरी, माँ धारण कीन्हो।
हीरा पन्ना दमके, तन श्रृंगार लीन्हो॥

Sundar chīr sunahrī, mān dhāran kīnho |
Hīrā pannā damke, tan shringār līnho ||

The Mother wears a beautiful golden sari; diamonds and emeralds sparkle on her adorned form.

॥ जय सन्तोषी माता, मैया जय सन्तोषी माता ॥Jai Santoshī mātā, maiyā jai Santoshī mātā

गेरू लाल छटा छवि, बदन कमल सोहे।
मंद हँसत करुणामयी, त्रिभुवन जन मोहे॥

Gerū lāl chhatā chhavi, badan kamal sohe |
Mand hansat karunāmayī, tribhuvan jan mohe ||

Her face glows like a lotus with a red-ochre radiance; her gentle, compassionate smile enchants the three worlds.

॥ जय सन्तोषी माता, मैया जय सन्तोषी माता ॥Jai Santoshī mātā, maiyā jai Santoshī mātā

स्वर्ण सिंहासन बैठी, चंवर ढुरें प्यारे।
धूप, दीप, मधु, मेवा, भोग धरें न्यारे॥

Svarna sinhāsan baithī, chanvar dhuren pyāre |
Dhūp, dīp, madhu, mevā, bhog dharen nyāre ||

She sits on a golden throne as whisks are lovingly waved; incense, lamps, honey and dry fruits are set before her.

॥ जय सन्तोषी माता, मैया जय सन्तोषी माता ॥Jai Santoshī mātā, maiyā jai Santoshī mātā

गुड़ अरु चना परमप्रिय, तामें संतोष कियो।
संतोषी कहलाई, भक्तन वैभव दियो॥

Gud aru chanā parampriy, tāmen santosh kiyo |
Santoshī kahlāī, bhaktan vaibhav diyo ||

Jaggery and chana are dearest to her, and she was content with them; so she is called Santoshi, and she gives abundance to her devotees.

॥ जय सन्तोषी माता, मैया जय सन्तोषी माता ॥Jai Santoshī mātā, maiyā jai Santoshī mātā

शुक्रवार प्रिय मानत, आज दिवस सोही।
भक्त मंडली छाई, कथा सुनत मोही॥

Shukravār priy mānat, āj divas sohī |
Bhakt mandalī chhāī, kathā sunat mohī ||

Friday is her favourite day, and today is that day; her devotees have gathered and are absorbed in hearing her story.

॥ जय सन्तोषी माता, मैया जय सन्तोषी माता ॥Jai Santoshī mātā, maiyā jai Santoshī mātā

मंदिर जगमग ज्योति, मंगल ध्वनि छाई।
विनय करें हम सेवक, चरनन सिर नाई॥

Mandir jagmag jyoti, mangal dhvani chhāī |
Vinay karen ham sevak, charnan sir nāī ||

The temple glitters with light and auspicious sounds fill the air; we, your servants, bow our heads at your feet and pray.

॥ जय सन्तोषी माता, मैया जय सन्तोषी माता ॥Jai Santoshī mātā, maiyā jai Santoshī mātā

भक्ति भावमय पूजा, अंगीकृत कीजै।
जो मन बसे हमारे, इच्छा फल दीजै॥

Bhakti bhāvmay pūjā, angīkrit kījai |
Jo man base hamāre, ichchhā phal dījai ||

Accept this worship offered with devotion, and grant the wish that lives in our hearts.

॥ जय सन्तोषी माता, मैया जय सन्तोषी माता ॥Jai Santoshī mātā, maiyā jai Santoshī mātā

दुखी, दरिद्री, रोगी, संकटमुक्त किए।
बहु धनधान्य भरे घर, सुख सौभाग्य दिए॥

Dukhī, daridrī, rogī, sankatmukt kie |
Bahu dhandhānya bhare ghar, sukh saubhāgya die ||

You have freed the sorrowful, the poor and the sick from their troubles, filled homes with wealth and grain, and given joy and good fortune.

॥ जय सन्तोषी माता, मैया जय सन्तोषी माता ॥Jai Santoshī mātā, maiyā jai Santoshī mātā

ध्यान धर्यो जिस जन ने, मनवांछित फल पायो।
पूजा कथा श्रवण कर, घर आनंद आयो॥

Dhyān dharyo jis jan ne, manvānchhit phal pāyo |
Pūjā kathā shravan kar, ghar ānand āyo ||

Whoever meditated on you received what the heart desired; by worshipping and hearing your story, joy came into the home.

॥ जय सन्तोषी माता, मैया जय सन्तोषी माता ॥Jai Santoshī mātā, maiyā jai Santoshī mātā

शरण गहे की लज्जा, राखियो जगदंबे।
संकट तू ही निवारे, दयामयी अंबे॥

Sharan gahe kī lajjā, rākhiyo Jagdambe |
Sankat tū hī nivāre, dayāmayī Ambe ||

O Mother of the world, protect the honour of those who take refuge in you; you alone remove our troubles, O compassionate Amba.

॥ जय सन्तोषी माता, मैया जय सन्तोषी माता ॥Jai Santoshī mātā, maiyā jai Santoshī mātā

संतोषी माँ की आरती, जो कोई नर गावे।
ऋद्धि-सिद्धि सुख संपत्ति, जी भरकर पावे॥

Santoshī mān kī ārtī, jo koī nar gāve |
Riddhi-siddhi sukh sampatti, jī bharkar pāve ||

Whoever sings this aarti of Santoshi Ma receives prosperity, success, happiness and wealth in full measure.

॥ जय सन्तोषी माता, मैया जय सन्तोषी माता ॥Jai Santoshī mātā, maiyā jai Santoshī mātā

The refrain ॥ जय सन्तोषी माता, मैया जय सन्तोषी माता ॥ is sung after every stanza, as marked.
