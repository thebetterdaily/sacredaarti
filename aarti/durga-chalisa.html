12 · CHALISA

Shri Durga Chalisa

श्री दुर्गा चालीसा

DurgaDeity
ChalisaForm
Friday, TuesdayDay
Navratri, Durga PujaOccasion

Forty verses to the Divine Mother, signed by the poet Devidas. It names her many forms (Annapurna, Saraswati, Lakshmi, the ten Mahavidyas, Kali) and recalls the story of Adi Shankaracharya realising that all power (shakti) is hers.

Chaupai 1–4

नमो नमो दुर्गे सुख करनी। नमो नमो अम्बे दुःख हरनी॥
निरंकार है ज्योति तुम्हारी। तिहूँ लोक फैली उजियारी॥
शशि ललाट मुख महाविशाला। नेत्र लाल भृकुटि विकराला॥
रूप मातु को अधिक सुहावे। दरश करत जन अति सुख पावे॥

Namo namo Durge sukh karnī | Namo namo Ambe dukh harnī ||
Nirankār hai jyoti tumhārī | Tihūn lok phailī ujiyārī ||
Shashi lalāt mukh mahāvishālā | Netra lāl bhrikuti vikrālā ||
Rūp mātu ko adhik suhāve | Darash karat jan ati sukh pāve ||

Salutations to Durga, giver of happiness; salutations to Ambe, remover of sorrow. Your light is formless and illumines the three worlds. A moon on your brow, your face is radiant, your eyes red and your brows fierce. Mother, your form is so lovely that people are filled with joy at seeing you.

Chaupai 5–8

तुम संसार शक्ति लय कीना। पालन हेतु अन्न धन दीना॥
अन्नपूर्णा हुई जग पाला। तुम ही आदि सुन्दरी बाला॥
प्रलयकाल सब नाशन हारी। तुम गौरी शिवशंकर प्यारी॥
शिव योगी तुम्हरे गुण गावें। ब्रह्मा विष्णु तुम्हें नित ध्यावें॥

Tum sansār shakti lay kīnā | Pālan hetu anna dhan dīnā ||
Annapūrnā huī jag pālā | Tum hī ādi sundarī bālā ||
Pralaykāl sab nāshan hārī | Tum Gaurī shivshankar pyārī ||
Shiv yogī tumhare gun gāven | Brahmā Vishnu tumhen nit dhyāven ||

All the power of the world rests in you; you give food and wealth to sustain it. As Annapurna you nourish the world; you are the primal, beautiful young goddess. At the time of dissolution you destroy all; you are Gauri, beloved of Shiva. Shiva and the yogis sing your virtues, and Brahma and Vishnu meditate on you always.

Chaupai 9–12

रूप सरस्वती को तुम धारा। दे सुबुद्धि ऋषि मुनिन उबारा॥
धरयो रूप नरसिंह को अम्बा। परगट भई फाड़कर खम्बा॥
रक्षा करि प्रह्लाद बचायो। हिरणाकुश को स्वर्ग पठायो॥
लक्ष्मी रूप धरो जग माहीं। श्री नारायण अंग समाहीं॥

Rūp Saraswatī ko tum dhārā | De subuddhi rishi munin ubārā ||
Dharyo rūp narsinh ko Ambā | Pargat bhaī phādkar khambā ||
Rakshā kari Prahlād bachāyo | Hirnākush ko svarg pathāyo ||
Lakshmī rūp dharo jag māhīn | Shrī Nārāyan ang samāhīn ||

Taking the form of Saraswati you gave wisdom to sages and saved them. Mother, you took the form of Narasimha and burst out of the pillar, protecting Prahlada and sending Hiranyakashipu to heaven. You took the form of Lakshmi in the world and dwell with Shri Narayan.

Chaupai 13–16

क्षीरसिन्धु में करत विलासा। दयासिन्धु दीजै मन आसा॥
हिंगलाज में तुम्हीं भवानी। महिमा अमित न जात बखानी॥
मातंगी अरु धूमावति माता। भुवनेश्वरी बगला सुख दाता॥
श्री भैरव तारा जग तारिणी। छिन्न भाल भव दुःख निवारिणी॥

Kshīrasindhu mein karat vilāsā | Dayāsindhu dījai man āsā ||
Hingalāj mein tumhīn Bhavānī | Mahimā amit na jāt bakhānī ||
Mātangī aru dhūmāvti mātā | Bhuvneshvarī baglā sukh dātā ||
Shrī bhairav tārā jag tārinī | Chhinna bhāl bhav dukh nivārinī ||

You rest in the ocean of milk: ocean of mercy, fulfil the hope of my heart. You are Bhavani at Hinglaj; your boundless glory cannot be described. You are Matangi and Dhumavati, Bhuvaneshwari and Bagala, givers of happiness; Bhairavi and Tara who carry the world across, and Chhinnamasta who ends worldly sorrow.

Chaupai 17–20

केहरि वाहन सोह भवानी। लांगुर वीर चलत अगवानी॥
कर में खप्पर खड्ग विराजै। जाको देख काल डर भाजै॥
सोहै अस्त्र और त्रिशूला। जाते उठत शत्रु हिय शूला॥
नगरकोट में तुम्हीं विराजत। तिहुँलोक में डंका बाजत॥

Kehari vāhan soh Bhavānī | Lāngur vīr chalat agvānī ||
Kar mein khappar khadg virājai | Jāko dekh kāl dar bhājai ||
Sohai astra aur trishūlā | Jāte uthat shatru hiy shūlā ||
Nagarkot mein tumhīn virājat | Tihunlok mein dankā bājat ||

Bhavani looks magnificent on her lion, with the brave Langur (Hanuman) leading the way. A skull-bowl and sword are in your hand; seeing them, even Death runs in fear. Your weapons and trident shine, striking pain into the hearts of enemies. You dwell at Nagarkot, and your drum resounds through the three worlds.

Chaupai 21–24

शुंभ निशुंभ दानव तुम मारे। रक्तबीज शंखन संहारे॥
महिषासुर नृप अति अभिमानी। जेहि अघ भार मही अकुलानी॥
रूप कराल कालिका धारा। सेन सहित तुम तिहि संहारा॥
परी गाढ़ संतन पर जब जब। भई सहाय मातु तुम तब तब॥

Shumbh nishumbh dānav tum māre | Raktabīj shankhan sanhāre ||
Mahishāsur nrip ati abhimānī | Jehi agh bhār mahī akulānī ||
Rūp karāl kālikā dhārā | Sen sahit tum tihi sanhārā ||
Parī gādh santan par jab jab | Bhaī sahāy mātu tum tab tab ||

You killed the demons Shumbha and Nishumbha and destroyed countless Raktabijas. King Mahishasura was so arrogant that the earth groaned under his sins; taking the fierce form of Kalika, you destroyed him and his army. Whenever hardship fell upon the saints, Mother, you came to help them.

Chaupai 25–28

अमरपुरी अरु बासव लोका। तब महिमा सब रहें अशोका॥
ज्वाला में है ज्योति तुम्हारी। तुम्हें सदा पूजें नर नारी॥
प्रेम भक्ति से जो यश गावें। दुःख दारिद्र निकट नहिं आवें॥
ध्यावे तुम्हें जो नर मन लाई। जन्म मरण ताकौ छुटि जाई॥

Amarpurī aru bāsav lokā | Tab mahimā sab rahen ashokā ||
Jvālā mein hai jyoti tumhārī | Tumhen sadā pūjen nar nārī ||
Prem bhakti se jo yash gāven | Dukh dāridra nikat nahin āven ||
Dhyāve tumhen jo nar man lāī | Janma maran tākau chhuti jāī ||

By your glory the city of the gods and Indra's realm remain free of sorrow. Your light burns at Jwala, where men and women worship you always. Sorrow and poverty never come near those who sing your praise with loving devotion. Whoever meditates on you with a focused mind is freed from birth and death.

Chaupai 29–32

जोगी सुर मुनि कहत पुकारी। योग न हो बिन शक्ति तुम्हारी॥
शंकर आचारज तप कीनो। काम अरु क्रोध जीति सब लीनो॥
निशिदिन ध्यान धरो शंकर को। काहु काल नहिं सुमिरो तुमको॥
शक्ति रूप का मरम न पायो। शक्ति गई तब मन पछितायो॥

Jogī sur muni kahat pukārī | Yog na ho bin shakti tumhārī ||
Shankar āchāraj tap kīno | Kām aru krodh jīti sab līno ||
Nishidin dhyān dharo Shankar ko | Kāhu kāl nahin sumiro tumko ||
Shakti rūp kā maram na pāyo | Shakti gaī tab man pachhitāyo ||

Yogis, gods and sages proclaim that no yoga is possible without your power. Shankaracharya performed austerities and conquered desire and anger. Day and night he meditated on Shiva, but never remembered you. He did not understand the secret of Shakti; when his power left him, he regretted it.

Chaupai 33–36

शरणागत हुई कीर्ति बखानी। जय जय जय जगदम्ब भवानी॥
भई प्रसन्न आदि जगदम्बा। दई शक्ति नहिं कीन विलम्बा॥
मोको मातु कष्ट अति घेरो। तुम बिन कौन हरै दुःख मेरो॥
आशा तृष्णा निपट सतावें। रिपु मूरख मोहि अति डर पावें॥

Sharnāgat huī kīrti bakhānī | Jai jai jai Jagdamb Bhavānī ||
Bhaī prasanna ādi Jagdambā | Daī shakti nahin kīn vilambā ||
Moko mātu kasht ati ghero | Tum bin kaun harai dukh mero ||
Āshā trishnā nipat satāven | Ripu mūrakh mohi ati dar pāven ||

He took refuge in you and sang your glory: 'Victory to Jagadamba Bhavani!' The primal Mother was pleased and restored his power without delay. Mother, I am surrounded by troubles; who but you can remove my sorrow? Hope and craving torment me, and foolish enemies frighten me.

Chaupai 37–40

शत्रु नाश कीजै महारानी। सुमिरौं इकचित तुम्हें भवानी॥
करो कृपा हे मातु दयाला। ऋद्धि सिद्धि दै करहु निहाला॥
जब लगि जिऊँ दया फल पाऊँ। तुम्हरो यश मैं सदा सुनाऊँ॥
दुर्गा चालीसा जो कोई गावै। सब सुख भोग परमपद पावै॥
देवीदास शरण निज जानी। करहु कृपा जगदम्ब भवानी॥

Shatru nāsh kījai mahārānī | Sumiraun ikchit tumhen Bhavānī ||
Karo kripā he mātu dayālā | Riddhi siddhi dai karahu nihālā ||
Jab lagi jiūn dayā phal pāūn | Tumharo yash main sadā sunāūn ||
Durgā chālīsā jo koī gāvai | Sab sukh bhog parampad pāvai ||
Devīdās sharan nij jānī | Karahu kripā Jagdamb Bhavānī ||

Great Queen, destroy my enemies; Bhavani, I remember you single-mindedly. Kind Mother, show your grace and bless me with prosperity and success. As long as I live, may I receive your mercy and always tell of your glory. Whoever sings this Durga Chalisa enjoys every happiness and reaches the highest state. Knowing Devidas to be in your shelter, show him your grace, Jagadamba Bhavani.
