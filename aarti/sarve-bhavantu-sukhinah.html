40 · MANTRA

Sarve Bhavantu Sukhinah

सर्वे भवन्तु सुखिनः

UniversalDeity
MantraForm
DailyDay
End of puja, Prayer meetingsOccasion
ॐ

A traditional prayer for the welfare of all beings, often recited at the close of worship or a gathering.

ॐ सर्वे भवन्तु सुखिनः सर्वे सन्तु निरामयाः।
सर्वे भद्राणि पश्यन्तु मा कश्चिद्दुःखभाग्भवेत्।
ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः॥

Om sarve bhavantu sukhinah sarve santu nirāmayāh |
Sarve bhadrāni pashyantu mā kashchidduhkhabhāgbhavet |
Om shāntih shāntih shāntih ||

May all be happy; may all be free from illness; may all see what is good; may no one suffer. Om, peace, peace, peace.
