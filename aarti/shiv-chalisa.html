11 · CHALISA

Shri Shiv Chalisa

श्री शिव चालीसा

ShivaDeity
ChalisaForm
MondayDay
Mahashivratri, Shravan MondaysOccasion

Forty verses in praise of Shiva, signed by the poet Ayodhyadas. It recalls Shiva's family on Kailash, his drinking of the poison at the churning of the ocean (which made him Neelkanth, the blue-throated) and his victories over Tripurasura and Jalandhara.

Doha

जय गणेश गिरिजा सुवन, मंगल मूल सुजान।
कहत अयोध्यादास तुम, देहु अभय वरदान॥

Jai Ganesh Girijā suvan, mangal mūl sujān |
Kahat Ayodhyādās tum, dehu abhay vardān ||

Glory to Ganesha, wise son of Girija and root of all blessings. Ayodhyadas prays: grant me the boon of fearlessness.

Chaupai 1–4

जय गिरिजा पति दीन दयाला। सदा करत सन्तन प्रतिपाला॥
भाल चन्द्रमा सोहत नीके। कानन कुण्डल नागफनी के॥
अंग गौर शिर गंग बहाये। मुण्डमाल तन क्षार लगाए॥
वस्त्र खाल बाघम्बर सोहे। छवि को देखि नाग मन मोहे॥

Jai Girijā pati dīn dayālā | Sadā karat santan pratipālā ||
Bhāl chandramā sohat nīke | Kānan kundal nāgphanī ke ||
Ang gaur shir gang bahāye | Mundamāl tan kshār lagāe ||
Vastra khāl bāghambar sohe | Chhavi ko dekhi nāg man mohe ||

Glory to the husband of Girija, kind to the humble, who always protects the saints. The moon shines on his brow and serpent hoods are his earrings. His body is fair, the Ganga flows from his head; he wears a garland of skulls and his body is smeared with ash. Clad in tiger skin, his beauty enchants even the serpents.

Chaupai 5–8

मैना मातु की हवे दुलारी। बाम अंग सोहत छवि न्यारी॥
कर त्रिशूल सोहत छवि भारी। करत सदा शत्रुन क्षयकारी॥
नन्दि गणेश सोहै तहँ कैसे। सागर मध्य कमल हैं जैसे॥
कार्तिक श्याम और गणराऊ। या छवि को कहि जात न काऊ॥

Mainā mātu kī have dulārī | Bām ang sohat chhavi nyārī ||
Kar trishūl sohat chhavi bhārī | Karat sadā shatrun kshayakārī ||
Nandi Ganesh sohai tahan kaise | Sāgar madhya kamal hain jaise ||
Kārtik shyām aur Ganrāū | Yā chhavi ko kahi jāt na kāū ||

Mother Maina's beloved daughter (Parvati) graces his left side with unique beauty. The trident in his hand looks magnificent and always destroys enemies. Nandi and Ganesha shine beside him like lotuses in the middle of the ocean. Kartikeya, Shyam and Ganesha: no one can fully describe this sight.

Chaupai 9–12

देवन जबहीं जाय पुकारा। तब ही दुख प्रभु आप निवारा॥
किया उपद्रव तारक भारी। देवन सब मिलि तुमहिं जुहारी॥
तुरत षडानन आप पठायउ। लवनिमेष महँ मारि गिरायउ॥
आप जलंधर असुर संहारा। सुयश तुम्हार विदित संसारा॥

Devan jabahīn jāy pukārā | Tab hī dukh prabhu āp nivārā ||
Kiyā upadrav tārak bhārī | Devan sab mili tumahin juhārī ||
Turat shadānan āp pathāyau | Lavnimesh mahan māri girāyau ||
Āp jalandhar asur sanhārā | Suyash tumhār vidit sansārā ||

Whenever the gods cried out to you, Lord, you removed their sorrow. When the demon Taraka caused great havoc, the gods together prayed to you. At once you sent six-faced Kartikeya, who struck him down in an instant. You destroyed the demon Jalandhara; your fame is known throughout the world.

Chaupai 13–16

त्रिपुरासुर सन युद्ध मचाई। सबहिं कृपा कर लीन बचाई॥
किया तपहिं भागीरथ भारी। पुरब प्रतिज्ञा तासु पुरारी॥
दानिन महँ तुम सम कोउ नाहीं। सेवक स्तुति करत सदाहीं॥
वेद नाम महिमा तव गाई। अकथ अनादि भेद नहिं पाई॥

Tripurāsur san yuddh machāī | Sabahin kripā kar līn bachāī ||
Kiyā tapahin bhāgīrath bhārī | Purab pratigyā tāsu purārī ||
Dānin mahan tum sam kou nāhīn | Sevak stuti karat sadāhīn ||
Ved nām mahimā tav gāī | Akath anādi bhed nahin pāī ||

You fought the demon Tripura and graciously saved everyone. Bhagiratha performed great austerity, and you fulfilled his vow (by receiving the Ganga). Among givers there is none like you; your servants always sing your praise. The Vedas sing the glory of your name, yet even they cannot fathom you, the beginningless and indescribable.

Chaupai 17–20

प्रकटी उदधि मंथन में ज्वाला। जरत सुरासुर भए विहाला॥
कीन्ही दया तहं करी सहाई। नीलकण्ठ तब नाम कहाई॥
पूजन रामचन्द्र जब कीन्हा। जीत के लंक विभीषण दीन्हा॥
सहस कमल में हो रहे धारी। कीन्ह परीक्षा तबहिं पुरारी॥

Prakatī udadhi manthan mein jvālā | Jarat surāsur bhae vihālā ||
Kīnhī dayā taham karī sahāī | Nīlkanth tab nām kahāī ||
Pūjan Rāmchandra jab kīnhā | Jīt ke lank Vibhīshan dīnhā ||
Sahas kamal mein ho rahe dhārī | Kīnh parīkshā tabahin purārī ||

When poison blazed up from the churning of the ocean, gods and demons were in agony. You showed mercy and helped them, and so became known as Neelkanth. When Ramachandra worshipped you, he won Lanka and gave it to Vibhishana. As he offered a thousand lotuses, you put him to the test.

Chaupai 21–24

एक कमल प्रभु राखेउ जोई। कमल नयन पूजन चहं सोई॥
कठिन भक्ति देखी प्रभु शंकर। भए प्रसन्न दिए इच्छित वर॥
जय जय जय अनन्त अविनाशी। करत कृपा सब के घटवासी॥
दुष्ट सकल नित मोहि सतावै। भ्रमत रहौं मोहि चैन न आवै॥

Ek kamal prabhu rākheu joī | Kamal nayan pūjan chaham soī ||
Kathin bhakti dekhī prabhu Shankar | Bhae prasanna die ichchhit var ||
Jai jai jai anant avināshī | Karat kripā sab ke ghatvāsī ||
Dusht sakal nit mohi satāvai | Bhramat rahaun mohi chain na āvai ||

You hid one lotus, and Rama, the lotus-eyed, wished to offer his own eye in its place. Seeing such firm devotion, Lord Shankar was pleased and granted his wish. Victory to the infinite and imperishable one, who dwells in every heart and blesses all. The wicked trouble me constantly; I wander about and find no peace.

Chaupai 25–28

त्राहि त्राहि मैं नाथ पुकारो। येहि अवसर मोहि आन उबारो॥
लै त्रिशूल शत्रुन को मारो। संकट से मोहि आन उबारो॥
मात पिता भ्राता सब होई। संकट में पूछत नहिं कोई॥
स्वामी एक है आस तुम्हारी। आय हरहु मम संकट भारी॥

Trāhi trāhi main nāth pukāro | Yehi avsar mohi ān ubāro ||
Lai trishūl shatrun ko māro | Sankat se mohi ān ubāro ||
Māt pitā bhrātā sab hoī | Sankat mein pūchhat nahin koī ||
Swāmī ek hai ās tumhārī | Āy harahu mam sankat bhārī ||

'Save me, save me,' I call to you, Lord: come now and rescue me. Take up your trident, strike down my enemies and deliver me from trouble. Mother, father and brothers are all there, but in hard times no one asks after me. Lord, you are my only hope: come and take away my heavy burden.

Chaupai 29–32

धन निर्धन को देत सदा हीं। जो कोई जांचे सो फल पाहीं॥
अस्तुति केहि विधि करैं तुम्हारी। क्षमहु नाथ अब चूक हमारी॥
शंकर हो संकट के नाशन। मंगल कारण विघ्न विनाशन॥
योगी यति मुनि ध्यान लगावैं। शारद नारद शीश नवावैं॥

Dhan nirdhan ko det sadā hīn | Jo koī jānche so phal pāhīn ||
Astuti kehi vidhi karain tumhārī | Kshamahu nāth ab chūk hamārī ||
Shankar ho sankat ke nāshan | Mangal kāran vighna vināshan ||
Yogī yati muni dhyān lagāvain | Shārad Nārad shīsh navāvain ||

You always give wealth to the poor; whoever asks of you receives. How can we praise you properly? Lord, forgive our mistakes. Shankar, you destroy troubles, bring blessings and remove obstacles. Yogis, ascetics and sages meditate on you; Sharada and Narada bow their heads to you.

Chaupai 33–36

नमो नमो जय नमः शिवाय। सुर ब्रह्मादिक पार न पाय॥
जो यह पाठ करे मन लाई। ता पर होत है शम्भु सहाई॥
ऋनियाँ जो कोई हो अधिकारी। पाठ करे सो पावन हारी॥
पुत्र होन कर इच्छा जोई। निश्चय शिव प्रसाद तेहि होई॥

Namo namo jai namah shivāy | Sur brahmādik pār na pāy ||
Jo yah pāth kare man lāī | Tā par hot hai Shambhu sahāī ||
Riniyān jo koī ho adhikārī | Pāth kare so pāvan hārī ||
Putra hon kar ichchhā joī | Nishchay Shiv prasād tehi hoī ||

Salutations again and again: Om Namah Shivaya. Even the gods and Brahma cannot reach your limits. Whoever recites this with a focused mind receives Shambhu's help. One burdened with debt who recites it is purified and freed. Whoever wishes for a child surely receives Shiva's blessing.

Chaupai 37–40

पण्डित त्रयोदशी को लावे। ध्यान पूर्वक होम करावे॥
त्रयोदशी व्रत करै हमेशा। ताके तन नहीं रहै कलेशा॥
धूप दीप नैवेद्य चढ़ावे। शंकर सम्मुख पाठ सुनावे॥
जन्म जन्म के पाप नसावे। अन्त धाम शिवपुर में पावे॥
कहैं अयोध्यादास आस तुम्हारी। जानि सकल दुःख हरहु हमारी॥

Pandit trayodashī ko lāve | Dhyān pūrvak hom karāve ||
Trayodashī vrat karai hameshā | Tāke tan nahīn rahai kaleshā ||
Dhūp dīp naivedya chadhāve | Shankar sammukh pāth sunāve ||
Janma janma ke pāp nasāve | Ant dhām shivpur mein pāve ||
Kahain Ayodhyādās ās tumhārī | Jāni sakal dukh harahu hamārī ||

Invite a priest on Trayodashi and have a havan performed with devotion. Whoever keeps the Trayodashi fast regularly is free of bodily suffering. Offer incense, lamp and food, and recite this before Shankar: the sins of many lives are destroyed and one reaches Shiva's abode at the end. Ayodhyadas says: you are my hope; knowing this, take away all our sorrows.

Doha

नित्त नेम कर प्रातः ही, पाठ करौं चालीसा।
तुम मेरी मनोकामना, पूर्ण करो जगदीश॥
मगसर छठि हेमन्त ऋतु, संवत चौसठ जान।
अस्तुति चालीसा शिवहि, पूर्ण कीन कल्याण॥

Nitt nem kar prātah hī, pāth karaun chālīsā |
Tum merī manokāmnā, pūrna karo Jagdīsh ||
Magsar chhathi hemant ritu, sanvat chausath jān |
Astuti chālīsā shivahi, pūrna kīn kalyān ||

Each morning, as a daily discipline, I recite this Chalisa: Lord of the world, fulfil my heart's wish. Know that this hymn of forty verses to Shiva was completed in the winter season, on the sixth day of Margashirsha, in Samvat (year) 64, for the welfare of all.
