13 · STUTI

Shri Ramchandra Kripalu

श्री रामचन्द्र कृपालु भजु मन

RamDeity
StutiForm
DailyDay
Ram Navami, DiwaliOccasion

A stuti (hymn of praise) by Goswami Tulsidas from the Vinay Patrika, in highly Sanskritised Hindi. It is commonly sung as the Ram aarti. Many singers continue with the verses from Bal Kand of the Ramcharitmanas (Gauri's blessing to Sita), included here as they are usually sung.

श्री रामचन्द्र कृपालु भजु मन, हरण भवभय दारुणं।
नवकंज लोचन कंज मुख, कर कंज पद कंजारुणं॥

Shrī Rāmchandra kripālu bhaju man, haran bhavbhay dārunam |
Navkanj lochan kanj mukh, kar kanj pad kanjārunam ||

O mind, worship the merciful Shri Ramachandra, who removes the terrible fear of worldly existence. His eyes are like fresh lotuses, his face, hands and feet are lotus-like and rosy.

कन्दर्प अगणित अमित छवि, नवनील नीरद सुन्दरं।
पट पीत मानहुँ तड़ित रुचि, शुचि नौमि जनक सुतावरं॥

Kandarp agnit amit chhavi, navnīl nīrad sundaram |
Pat pīt mānhun tadit ruchi, shuchi naumi janak sutāvaram ||

His beauty surpasses countless Kamadevas; he is lovely as a fresh dark rain cloud. His yellow garment gleams like lightning: I bow to the pure husband of Janaka's daughter.

भजु दीनबन्धु दिनेश दानव, दैत्य वंश निकन्दनं।
रघुनन्द आनन्द कन्द कोशल, चन्द दशरथ नन्दनं॥

Bhaju dīnbandhu dinesh dānav, daitya vansh nikandanam |
Raghunand ānand kand koshal, chand Dasharath nandanam ||

Worship the friend of the humble, radiant as the sun, destroyer of the demon race; the joy of the Raghus, root of bliss, moon of Kosala, son of Dasharatha.

सिर मुकुट कुण्डल तिलक चारु, उदारु अंग विभूषणं।
आजानु भुज शर चाप धर, संग्राम जित खरदूषणं॥

Sir mukut kundal tilak chāru, udāru ang vibhūshanam |
Ājānu bhuj shar chāp dhar, sangrām jit khardūshanam ||

A crown on his head, earrings and a lovely tilak, his noble limbs adorned with ornaments; his arms reach to his knees, he holds bow and arrows, and he conquered Khara and Dushana in battle.

इति वदति तुलसीदास शंकर, शेष मुनि मन रंजनं।
मम हृदय कंज निवास कुरु, कामादि खल दल गंजनं॥

Iti vadti Tulsīdās Shankar, shesh muni man ranjanam |
Mam hriday kanj nivās kuru, kāmādi khal dal ganjanam ||

So says Tulsidas: O delight of the hearts of Shankar, Shesha and the sages, make your home in the lotus of my heart and destroy the wicked army of desire and the other passions.

Bal Kand

मनु जाहिं राचेउ मिलिहि सो बरु, सहज सुन्दर साँवरो।
करुना निधान सुजान सीलु, सनेहु जानत रावरो॥

Manu jāhin rācheu milihi so baru, sahaj sundar sānvaro |
Karunā nidhān sujān sīlu, sanehu jānat rāvro ||

(Gauri blesses Sita:) The one your heart is set on, naturally handsome and dark, will be your husband; that treasury of compassion, wise and gentle, knows your love.

Bal Kand

एहि भाँति गौरि असीस सुनि सिय, सहित हियँ हरषीं अली।
तुलसी भवानिहि पूजि पुनि पुनि, मुदित मन मन्दिर चली॥

Ehi bhānti gauri asīs suni siy, sahit hiyan harshīn alī |
Tulsī bhavānihi pūji puni puni, mudit man mandir chalī ||

Hearing Gauri's blessing, Sita and her companions rejoiced. Says Tulsi: worshipping Bhavani again and again, she returned to the palace with a joyful heart.

Soratha

जानि गौरि अनुकूल सिय हिय, हरषु न जाइ कहि।
मंजुल मंगल मूल बाम अंग, फरकन लगे॥

Jāni gauri anukūl siy hiy, harshu na jāi kahi |
Manjul mangal mūl bām ang, pharkan lage ||

Knowing Gauri was pleased, the joy in Sita's heart was beyond words, and her left side began to throb, a lovely sign of good fortune.
