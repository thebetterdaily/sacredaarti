33 · AARTI

Jai Jai Tulsi Mata

जय जय तुलसी माता

Tulsi MataDeity
AartiForm
DailyDay
Tulsi Vivah, Kartik monthOccasion

The aarti of Tulsi, the holy basil, worshipped as a goddess and as the beloved of Bhagwan Vishnu. Many homes light a lamp at the tulsi plant every evening, and the aarti is sung especially through the month of Kartik and at Tulsi Vivah.

जय जय तुलसी माता, मैया जय तुलसी माता।
सब जग की सुख दाता, वर दाता॥

Jai jai Tulsī mātā, maiyā jai Tulsī mātā |
Sab jag kī sukh dātā, var dātā ||

Victory to Mother Tulsi, who gives happiness and blessings to the whole world.

सब योगों के ऊपर, सब रोगों के ऊपर।
रज से रक्षा करके, भव त्राता॥

Sab yogon ke ūpar, sab rogon ke ūpar |
Raj se rakshā karke, bhav trātā ||

You are above every discipline and greater than every illness; you protect us from impurity and carry us across worldly life.

॥ जय जय तुलसी माता, मैया जय तुलसी माता ॥Jai jai Tulsī mātā, maiyā jai Tulsī mātā

बटु पुत्री हे श्यामा, सुर बल्ली हे ग्राम्या।
विष्णु प्रिये जो तुमको सेवे, सो नर तर जाता॥

Batu putrī he shyāmā, sur ballī he grāmyā |
Vishnu priye jo tumko seve, so nar tar jātā ||

O dark-leaved one, divine creeper who lives among the homes of the village: beloved of Bhagwan Vishnu, whoever serves you is carried across.

॥ जय जय तुलसी माता, मैया जय तुलसी माता ॥Jai jai Tulsī mātā, maiyā jai Tulsī mātā

हरि के शीश विराजत, त्रिभुवन से हो वन्दित।
पतित जनो की तारिणी, विख्याता॥

Hari ke shīsh virājat, tribhuvan se ho vandit |
Patit jano kī tārinī, vikhyātā ||

You rest on the head of Hari and are honoured by the three worlds; you are famed as the saviour of the fallen.

॥ जय जय तुलसी माता, मैया जय तुलसी माता ॥Jai jai Tulsī mātā, maiyā jai Tulsī mātā

लेकर जन्म विजन में, आई दिव्य भवन में।
मानव लोक तुम्ही से, सुख संपति पाता॥

Lekar janma vijan mein, āī divya bhavan mein |
Mānav lok tumhī se, sukh sampati pātā ||

Born in the wild, you came to live in sacred homes; through you the human world receives happiness and prosperity.

॥ जय जय तुलसी माता, मैया जय तुलसी माता ॥Jai jai Tulsī mātā, maiyā jai Tulsī mātā

हरि को तुम अति प्यारी, श्यामवरण तुम्हारी।
प्रेम अजब है उनका, तुमसे कैसा नाता॥

Hari ko tum ati pyārī, shyāmvaran tumhārī |
Prem ajab hai unkā, tumse kaisā nātā ||

You are most dear to Hari, and your colour is his dark hue; how wondrous is his love, and how deep his bond with you.

॥ जय जय तुलसी माता, मैया जय तुलसी माता ॥Jai jai Tulsī mātā, maiyā jai Tulsī mātā

The refrain ॥ जय जय तुलसी माता, मैया जय तुलसी माता ॥ is sung after every stanza, as marked.
