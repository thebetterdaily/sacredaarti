23 · AARTI

Jai Lakshmi Ramana (Satyanarayan Aarti)

जय लक्ष्मी रमणा

SatyanarayanDeity
AartiForm
ThursdayDay
Satyanarayan Puja, PurnimaOccasion

Sung at the close of the Satyanarayan Katha, usually on Purnima (full moon) or on happy occasions such as a house-warming. Its verses recall characters from the katha itself: the poor Brahmin, the woodcutter, King Chandrachud and the merchant.

ॐ जय लक्ष्मी रमणा, स्वामी जय लक्ष्मी रमणा।
सत्यनारायण स्वामी, जन पातक हरणा॥

Om jai Lakshmī ramanā, swāmī jai Lakshmī ramanā |
Satyanārāyan swāmī, jan pātak harnā ||

Glory to the beloved of Lakshmi, Lord Satyanarayan, who takes away the sins of the people.

रत्न जड़ित सिंहासन, अद्भुत छवि राजे।
नारद करत निरन्तर, घण्टा ध्वनि बाजे॥

Ratna jadit sinhāsan, adbhut chhavi rāje |
Nārad karat nirantar, ghantā dhvani bāje ||

You sit on a jewelled throne in wondrous splendour; Narada sings without pause and bells ring out.

॥ ॐ जय लक्ष्मी रमणा ॥Om jai Lakshmī ramanā

प्रकट भये कलि कारण, द्विज को दरस दियो।
बूढ़ो ब्राह्मण बनकर, कंचन महल कियो॥

Prakat bhaye kali kāran, dvij ko daras diyo |
Būdho brāhman bankar, kanchan mahal kiyo ||

You appeared for the sake of this Kali age and gave darshan to a Brahmin; disguised as an old Brahmin, you turned his home into a golden palace.

॥ ॐ जय लक्ष्मी रमणा ॥Om jai Lakshmī ramanā

दुर्बल भील कठारो, जिन पर कृपा करी।
चन्द्रचूड़ एक राजा, जिनकी विपति हरी॥

Durbal bhīl kathāro, jin par kripā karī |
Chandrachūd ek rājā, jinkī vipati harī ||

You showed grace to a poor woodcutter, and removed the misfortune of King Chandrachud.

॥ ॐ जय लक्ष्मी रमणा ॥Om jai Lakshmī ramanā

वैश्य मनोरथ पायो, श्रद्धा तज दीनी।
सो फल भोग्यो प्रभुजी, फिर स्तुति कीनी॥

Vaishya manorath pāyo, shraddhā taj dīnī |
So phal bhogyo prabhujī, phir stuti kīnī ||

The merchant got his wish but then abandoned his faith; he suffered the result, and then praised you again.

॥ ॐ जय लक्ष्मी रमणा ॥Om jai Lakshmī ramanā

भाव भक्ति के कारण, छिन छिन रूप धरयो।
श्रद्धा धारण कीनी, तिनको काज सरयो॥

Bhāv bhakti ke kāran, chhin chhin rūp dharyo |
Shraddhā dhāran kīnī, tinko kāj saryo ||

For the sake of loving devotion you take form again and again; whoever holds on to faith has their work fulfilled.

॥ ॐ जय लक्ष्मी रमणा ॥Om jai Lakshmī ramanā

ग्वाल बाल संग राजा, बन में भक्ति करी।
मनवांछित फल दीन्हो, दीनदयाल हरी॥

Gvāl bāl sang rājā, ban mein bhakti karī |
Manvānchhit phal dīnho, dīndayāl harī ||

The cowherd boys and the king worshipped you in the forest, and Hari, kind to the humble, granted their wishes.

॥ ॐ जय लक्ष्मी रमणा ॥Om jai Lakshmī ramanā

चढ़त प्रसाद सवायो, कदली फल मेवा।
धूप दीप तुलसी से, राजी सत्यदेवा॥

Chadhat prasād savāyo, kadlī phal mevā |
Dhūp dīp Tulsī se, rājī Satyadevā ||

Offered prasad of one and a quarter measures, bananas and dry fruits, with incense, lamp and tulsi, the Lord of Truth is pleased.

॥ ॐ जय लक्ष्मी रमणा ॥Om jai Lakshmī ramanā

श्री सत्यनारायण जी की आरती, जो कोई नर गावे।
कहत शिवानन्द स्वामी, मनवांछित फल पावे॥

Shrī Satyanārāyan jī kī ārtī, jo koī nar gāve |
Kahat Shivānand swāmī, manvānchhit phal pāve ||

Swami Shivanand says: whoever sings this aarti of Shri Satyanarayan receives the fruit of their heart's wish.

॥ ॐ जय लक्ष्मी रमणा ॥Om jai Lakshmī ramanā

The refrain ॥ ॐ जय लक्ष्मी रमणा ॥ is sung after every stanza, as marked.
