48 · SHLOKA

Brahmarpanam (Before Meals)

ब्रह्मार्पणं ब्रह्म हविः

UniversalDeity
ShlokaForm
DailyDay
Before mealsOccasion
ॐ

Bhagavad Gita 4.24, recited before eating so that the meal itself becomes an offering.

ब्रह्मार्पणं ब्रह्म हविर्ब्रह्माग्नौ ब्रह्मणा हुतम्।
ब्रह्मैव तेन गन्तव्यं ब्रह्मकर्मसमाधिना॥

Brahmārpanam brahma havirbrahmāgnau brahmanā hutam |
Brahmaiva tena gantavyam brahmakarmasamādhinā ||

The act of offering is Brahman, the offering is Brahman, offered by Brahman into the fire of Brahman. One who sees Brahman in every action reaches Brahman.
