34 · SHLOKA

Manojavam Marutatulyavegam

मनोजवं मारुततुल्यवेगं

HanumanDeity
ShlokaForm
Tuesday, SaturdayDay
Hanuman JayantiOccasion

A short verse of surrender to Hanuman from the Ramaraksha Stotra, often recited before the Hanuman Chalisa.

मनोजवं मारुततुल्यवेगं जितेन्द्रियं बुद्धिमतां वरिष्ठम्।
वातात्मजं वानरयूथमुख्यं श्रीरामदूतं शरणं प्रपद्ये॥

Manojavam mārutatulyavegam jitendriyam buddhimatām varishtham |
Vātātmajam vānarayūthamukhyam shrīrāmadūtam sharanam prapadye ||

I take refuge in Shri Rama's messenger: swift as the mind and fast as the wind, master of the senses, foremost among the wise, son of the Wind and leader of the vanara army.
