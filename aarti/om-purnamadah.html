29 · MANTRA

Om Purnamadah Purnamidam

ॐ पूर्णमदः पूर्णमिदम्

UniversalDeity
MantraForm
DailyDay
Start or end of studyOccasion
ॐ

The peace invocation of the Isha Upanishad and Brihadaranyaka Upanishad, on the fullness of the Infinite.

ॐ पूर्णमदः पूर्णमिदं पूर्णात्पूर्णमुदच्यते।
पूर्णस्य पूर्णमादाय पूर्णमेवावशिष्यते॥
ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः॥

Om pūrnamadah pūrnamidam pūrnātpūrnamudachyate |
Pūrnasya pūrnamādāya pūrnamevāvashishyate ||
Om shāntih shāntih shāntih ||

That is whole; this is whole. From the whole, the whole arises. Taking the whole from the whole, the whole remains. Om, peace, peace, peace.
