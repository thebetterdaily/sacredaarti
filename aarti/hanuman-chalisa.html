03 · CHALISA

Shri Hanuman Chalisa

श्री हनुमान चालीसा

HanumanDeity
ChalisaForm
Tuesday, SaturdayDay
Hanuman Jayanti, TuesdayOccasion

Forty verses (chaupais) in Awadhi by Goswami Tulsidas (16th century), framed by opening and closing dohas. It is the most recited Hindu prayer in the world. Reciting it once a day, or seven or a hundred times on special occasions, is a common practice.

Doha

श्रीगुरु चरन सरोज रज, निज मनु मुकुरु सुधारि।
बरनउँ रघुबर बिमल जसु, जो दायकु फल चारि॥
बुद्धिहीन तनु जानिके, सुमिरौं पवन कुमार।
बल बुधि बिद्या देहु मोहिं, हरहु कलेस बिकार॥

Shrī guru charan saroj raj, nij manu mukuru sudhāri |
Barnaun Raghubar bimal jasu, jo dāyaku phal chāri ||
Buddhihīn tanu jānike, sumiraun Pavan kumār |
Bal budhi bidyā dehu mohin, harahu kales bikār ||

Cleaning the mirror of my mind with the dust of my Guru's lotus feet, I describe the pure glory of Rama, which grants the four fruits of life. Knowing myself to be lacking in wisdom, I remember you, son of the Wind: grant me strength, intelligence and knowledge, and remove my troubles and impurities.

Chaupai 1–4

जय हनुमान ज्ञान गुन सागर। जय कपीस तिहुँ लोक उजागर॥
राम दूत अतुलित बल धामा। अंजनि पुत्र पवनसुत नामा॥
महाबीर बिक्रम बजरंगी। कुमति निवार सुमति के संगी॥
कंचन बरन बिराज सुबेसा। कानन कुंडल कुंचित केसा॥

Jai Hanumān gyān gun sāgar | Jai kapīs tihun lok ujāgar ||
Rām dūt atulit bal dhāmā | Anjani putra pavansut nāmā ||
Mahābīr bikram bajrangī | Kumati nivār sumati ke sangī ||
Kanchan baran birāj subesā | Kānan kundal kunchit kesā ||

Victory to Hanuman, ocean of wisdom and virtue; victory to the lord of monkeys, famed in the three worlds. You are Rama's messenger, home of matchless strength, known as Anjani's son and son of the Wind. Great hero, mighty as a thunderbolt, you dispel bad thinking and befriend good sense. Your golden body is finely dressed, with earrings and curly hair.

Chaupai 5–8

हाथ बज्र औ ध्वजा बिराजै। काँधे मूँज जनेऊ साजै॥
संकर सुवन केसरीनंदन। तेज प्रताप महा जग बंदन॥
बिद्यावान गुनी अति चातुर। राम काज करिबे को आतुर॥
प्रभु चरित्र सुनिबे को रसिया। राम लखन सीता मन बसिया॥

Hāth bajra au dhvajā birājai | Kāndhe mūnj janeū sājai ||
Sankar suvan kesarīnandan | Tej pratāp mahā jag bandan ||
Bidyāvān gunī ati chātur | Rām kāj karibe ko ātur ||
Prabhu charitra sunibe ko rasiyā | Rām Lakhan Sītā man basiyā ||

A mace and a flag are in your hands and a sacred thread of munja grass on your shoulder. Embodiment of Shiva and son of Kesari, your radiance and glory are honoured by the whole world. Learned, virtuous and very clever, you are always eager to do Rama's work. You delight in hearing the Lord's story; Rama, Lakshmana and Sita live in your heart.

Chaupai 9–12

सूक्ष्म रूप धरि सियहिं दिखावा। बिकट रूप धरि लंक जरावा॥
भीम रूप धरि असुर सँहारे। रामचंद्र के काज सँवारे॥
लाय सजीवन लखन जियाये। श्रीरघुबीर हरषि उर लाये॥
रघुपति कीन्ही बहुत बड़ाई। तुम मम प्रिय भरतहि सम भाई॥

Sūkshma rūp dhari siyahin dikhāvā | Bikat rūp dhari lank jarāvā ||
Bhīm rūp dhari asur sanhāre | Rāmchandra ke kāj sanvāre ||
Lāy sajīvan Lakhan jiyāye | Shrī Raghubīr harashi ur lāye ||
Raghupati kīnhī bahut badāī | Tum mam priy bharatahi sam bhāī ||

Taking a tiny form you appeared before Sita; taking a fearsome form you burned Lanka. In a giant form you destroyed the demons and completed Rama's tasks. You brought the Sanjivani herb and revived Lakshmana, and Rama embraced you with joy. Rama praised you greatly: 'You are as dear to me as my brother Bharata.'

Chaupai 13–16

सहस बदन तुम्हरो जस गावैं। अस कहि श्रीपति कंठ लगावैं॥
सनकादिक ब्रह्मादि मुनीसा। नारद सारद सहित अहीसा॥
जम कुबेर दिगपाल जहाँ ते। कबि कोबिद कहि सके कहाँ ते॥
तुम उपकार सुग्रीवहिं कीन्हा। राम मिलाय राज पद दीन्हा॥

Sahas badan tumharo jas gāvain | As kahi Shrīpati kanth lagāvain ||
Sankādik brahmādi munīsā | Nārad Sārad sahit ahīsā ||
Jam Kuber digpāl jahān te | Kabi kobid kahi sake kahān te ||
Tum upkār Sugrīvahin kīnhā | Rām milāy rāj pad dīnhā ||

'May a thousand mouths sing your glory,' said Rama, embracing you. Sanaka and the sages, Brahma, Narada, Saraswati and the king of serpents; Yama, Kubera and the guardians of the directions: how could poets and scholars fully describe you? You did Sugriva a great service, bringing him to Rama and restoring his kingdom.

Chaupai 17–20

तुम्हरो मंत्र बिभीषन माना। लंकेस्वर भए सब जग जाना॥
जुग सहस्र जोजन पर भानू। लील्यो ताहि मधुर फल जानू॥
प्रभु मुद्रिका मेलि मुख माहीं। जलधि लाँघि गये अचरज नाहीं॥
दुर्गम काज जगत के जेते। सुगम अनुग्रह तुम्हरे तेते॥

Tumharo mantra Bibhīshan mānā | Lankesvar bhae sab jag jānā ||
Jug sahasra jojan par bhānū | Līlyo tāhi madhur phal jānū ||
Prabhu mudrikā meli mukh māhīn | Jaldhi lānghi gaye achraj nāhīn ||
Durgam kāj jagat ke jete | Sugam anugrah tumhare tete ||

Vibhishana heeded your advice and became king of Lanka, as the whole world knows. You swallowed the sun, thousands of miles away, thinking it a sweet fruit. Holding the Lord's ring in your mouth you leapt across the ocean; no wonder there. Every difficult task in the world becomes easy by your grace.

Chaupai 21–24

राम दुआरे तुम रखवारे। होत न आज्ञा बिनु पैसारे॥
सब सुख लहै तुम्हारी सरना। तुम रच्छक काहू को डर ना॥
आपन तेज सम्हारो आपै। तीनों लोक हाँक तें काँपै॥
भूत पिसाच निकट नहिं आवै। महाबीर जब नाम सुनावै॥

Rām duāre tum rakhvāre | Hot na āgyā binu paisāre ||
Sab sukh lahai tumhārī saranā | Tum rachchhak kāhū ko dar nā ||
Āpan tej samhāro āpai | Tīnon lok hānk ten kānpai ||
Bhūt pisāch nikat nahin āvai | Mahābīr jab nām sunāvai ||

You guard Rama's door; no one enters without your permission. All happiness is found in your shelter; with you as protector, there is nothing to fear. Only you can contain your own power; the three worlds tremble at your roar. Ghosts and evil spirits do not come near when your name, Mahavir, is spoken.

Chaupai 25–28

नासै रोग हरै सब पीरा। जपत निरंतर हनुमत बीरा॥
संकट तें हनुमान छुड़ावै। मन क्रम बचन ध्यान जो लावै॥
सब पर राम तपस्वी राजा। तिन के काज सकल तुम साजा॥
और मनोरथ जो कोई लावै। सोइ अमित जीवन फल पावै॥

Nāsai rog harai sab pīrā | Japat nirantar Hanumat bīrā ||
Sankat ten Hanumān chhudāvai | Man kram bachan dhyān jo lāvai ||
Sab par Rām tapasvī rājā | Tin ke kāj sakal tum sājā ||
Aur manorath jo koī lāvai | Soi amit jīvan phal pāvai ||

Disease is destroyed and all pain removed by constantly repeating the name of brave Hanuman. Hanuman frees from trouble those who remember him in thought, deed and word. Rama, the ascetic king, is above all, and you accomplished all his work. Whoever brings any other wish to you receives life's boundless reward.

Chaupai 29–32

चारों जुग परताप तुम्हारा। है परसिद्ध जगत उजियारा॥
साधु संत के तुम रखवारे। असुर निकंदन राम दुलारे॥
अष्ट सिद्धि नौ निधि के दाता। अस बर दीन जानकी माता॥
राम रसायन तुम्हरे पासा। सदा रहो रघुपति के दासा॥

Chāron jug partāp tumhārā | Hai parsiddh jagat ujiyārā ||
Sādhu sant ke tum rakhvāre | Asur nikandan Rām dulāre ||
Asht siddhi nau nidhi ke dātā | As bar dīn Jānkī mātā ||
Rām rasāyan tumhare pāsā | Sadā raho Raghupati ke dāsā ||

Your glory shines through all four ages and lights up the world. You protect saints and holy people, destroy demons and are dear to Rama. Mother Janaki gave you the boon to grant the eight siddhis and nine treasures. You hold the elixir of Rama's name and remain forever his servant.

Chaupai 33–36

तुम्हरे भजन राम को पावै। जनम जनम के दुख बिसरावै॥
अंत काल रघुबर पुर जाई। जहाँ जन्म हरि भक्त कहाई॥
और देवता चित्त न धरई। हनुमत सेइ सर्ब सुख करई॥
संकट कटै मिटै सब पीरा। जो सुमिरै हनुमत बलबीरा॥

Tumhare bhajan Rām ko pāvai | Janam janam ke dukh bisrāvai ||
Ant kāl Raghubar pur jāī | Jahān janma Hari bhakt kahāī ||
Aur devtā chitt na dharaī | Hanumat sei sarb sukh karaī ||
Sankat katai mitai sab pīrā | Jo sumirai Hanumat balbīrā ||

Through devotion to you one reaches Rama and forgets the sorrows of many lifetimes. At the end one goes to Rama's abode, and if born again is known as a devotee of Hari. Even without holding any other deity in mind, serving Hanuman brings every happiness. Troubles end and all pain vanishes for those who remember mighty Hanuman.

Chaupai 37–40

जै जै जै हनुमान गोसाईं। कृपा करहु गुरुदेव की नाईं॥
जो सत बार पाठ कर कोई। छूटहि बंदि महा सुख होई॥
जो यह पढ़ै हनुमान चालीसा। होय सिद्धि साखी गौरीसा॥
तुलसीदास सदा हरि चेरा। कीजै नाथ हृदय महँ डेरा॥

Jai jai jai Hanumān gosāīn | Kripā karahu gurudev kī nāīn ||
Jo sat bār pāth kar koī | Chhūtahi bandi mahā sukh hoī ||
Jo yah padhai Hanumān chālīsā | Hoy siddhi sākhī gaurīsā ||
Tulsīdās sadā Hari cherā | Kījai nāth hriday mahan derā ||

Victory, victory, victory to Lord Hanuman: bless me as a Guru would. Whoever recites this a hundred times is freed from bondage and finds great joy. Whoever reads this Hanuman Chalisa attains success; Shiva himself is witness. Tulsidas, forever Hari's servant, prays: Lord, make your home in my heart.

Doha

पवनतनय संकट हरन, मंगल मूरति रूप।
राम लखन सीता सहित, हृदय बसहु सुर भूप॥

Pavantanay sankat haran, mangal mūrati rūp |
Rām Lakhan Sītā sahit, hriday basahu sur bhūp ||

Son of the Wind, remover of troubles, embodiment of blessings: king of the gods, dwell in my heart together with Rama, Lakshmana and Sita.
