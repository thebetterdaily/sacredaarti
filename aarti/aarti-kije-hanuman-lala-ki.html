05 · AARTI

Aarti Kije Hanuman Lala Ki

आरती कीजै हनुमान लला की

HanumanDeity
AartiForm
Tuesday, SaturdayDay
Hanuman Jayanti, TuesdayOccasion

The standard Hanuman aarti, sung after the Hanuman Chalisa on Tuesdays and Saturdays. Its closing couplet names Tulsidas, the 16th-century poet of the Ramcharitmanas, and it retells the great deeds of Hanuman in the Ramayana.

आरती कीजै हनुमान लला की।
दुष्ट दलन रघुनाथ कला की॥

Ārtī kījai Hanumān lalā kī |
Dusht dalan Raghunāth kalā kī ||

Perform the aarti of dear Hanuman, who crushes the wicked and is a power of Lord Raghunath (Rama).

जाके बल से गिरिवर काँपे।
रोग दोष जाके निकट न झाँके॥
अंजनि पुत्र महा बलदाई।
सन्तन के प्रभु सदा सहाई॥

Jāke bal se girivar kānpe |
Rog dosh jāke nikat na jhānke ||
Anjani putra mahā baldāī |
Santan ke prabhu sadā sahāī ||

Mountains tremble at his strength, and disease and misfortune dare not come near him. Anjani's son gives great strength and is always the helper of saints.

॥ आरती कीजै हनुमान लला की ॥Ārtī kījai Hanumān lalā kī

दे बीरा रघुनाथ पठाए।
लंका जारि सिया सुधि लाए॥
लंका सो कोट समुद्र सी खाई।
जात पवनसुत बार न लाई॥

De bīrā Raghunāth pathāe |
Lankā jāri siyā sudhi lāe ||
Lankā so kot samudra sī khāī |
Jāt pavansut bār na lāī ||

Rama sent him with a token of trust; he burned Lanka and brought back news of Sita. Lanka was a fortress ringed by an ocean-wide moat, yet the son of the Wind crossed it without delay.

॥ आरती कीजै हनुमान लला की ॥Ārtī kījai Hanumān lalā kī

लंका जारि असुर संहारे।
सियारामजी के काज सँवारे॥
लक्ष्मण मूर्छित पड़े सकारे।
आनि संजीवन प्राण उबारे॥

Lankā jāri asur sanhāre |
Siyārāmjī ke kāj sanvāre ||
Lakshman mūrchhit pade sakāre |
Āni sanjīvan prān ubāre ||

He burned Lanka, destroyed the demons and accomplished the work of Sita and Rama. When Lakshmana lay unconscious, he brought the Sanjivani herb and saved his life.

॥ आरती कीजै हनुमान लला की ॥Ārtī kījai Hanumān lalā kī

पैठि पाताल तोरि जम कारे।
अहिरावण की भुजा उखारे॥
बाएँ भुजा असुर दल मारे।
दाहिने भुजा सन्तजन तारे॥

Paithi pātāl tori jam kāre |
Ahirāvan kī bhujā ukhāre ||
Bāen bhujā asur dal māre |
Dāhine bhujā santajan tāre ||

He entered the underworld, broke the bonds of death and tore off the arms of Ahiravana. With his left arm he struck down the demon army, and with his right he rescued the saints.

॥ आरती कीजै हनुमान लला की ॥Ārtī kījai Hanumān lalā kī

सुर नर मुनि आरती उतारें।
जय जय जय हनुमान उचारें॥
कंचन थार कपूर लौ छाई।
आरती करत अंजना माई॥

Sur nar muni ārtī utāren |
Jai jai jai Hanumān uchāren ||
Kanchan thār kapūr lau chhāī |
Ārtī karat Anjanā māī ||

Gods, people and sages wave the lamp for him and cry 'Victory to Hanuman!' With a golden plate glowing with camphor flame, Mother Anjana herself performs his aarti.

॥ आरती कीजै हनुमान लला की ॥Ārtī kījai Hanumān lalā kī

जो हनुमानजी की आरती गावे।
बसि बैकुण्ठ परम पद पावे॥
लंक विध्वंस किये रघुराई।
तुलसीदास स्वामी कीरति गाई॥

Jo Hanumānjī kī ārtī gāve |
Basi baikunth param pad pāve ||
Lank vidhvans kiye Raghurāī |
Tulsīdās swāmī kīrti gāī ||

Whoever sings Hanuman's aarti dwells in Vaikuntha and attains the highest state. Rama destroyed Lanka, and Tulsidas sings the glory of his Lord.

॥ आरती कीजै हनुमान लला की ॥Ārtī kījai Hanumān lalā kī

The refrain ॥ आरती कीजै हनुमान लला की ॥ is sung after every stanza, as marked.
