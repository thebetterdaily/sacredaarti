25 · AARTI

Jai Kashyap Nandan (Surya Aarti)

जय कश्यप नन्दन

SuryaDeity
AartiForm
SundayDay
Chhath Puja, Makar SankrantiOccasion

An aarti to Surya, the Sun, son of the sage Kashyapa and Aditi. It is sung on Sundays, at Makar Sankranti and in Chhath Puja, and asks the Sun to dispel both outer darkness and inner ignorance.

जय कश्यप नन्दन, ॐ जय अदिति नन्दन।
त्रिभुवन तिमिर निकन्दन, भक्त हृदय चन्दन॥

Jai Kashyap nandan, om jai Aditi nandan |
Tribhuvan timir nikandan, bhakt hriday chandan ||

Glory to the son of Kashyapa and Aditi, who destroys the darkness of the three worlds and is like cooling sandal to the devotee's heart.

सप्त अश्वरथ राजित, एक चक्रधारी।
दुःखहारी सुखकारी, मानस मल हारी॥

Sapt ashvarath rājit, ek chakradhārī |
Dukhhārī sukhkārī, mānas mal hārī ||

You ride a chariot of seven horses on a single wheel; you remove sorrow, give joy and cleanse the mind.

॥ ॐ जय कश्यप नन्दन ॥Om jai Kashyap nandan

सुर मुनि भूसुर वन्दित, विमल विभवशाली।
अघ दल दलन दिवाकर, दिव्य किरण माली॥

Sur muni bhūsur vandit, vimal vibhavshālī |
Agh dal dalan divākar, divya kiran mālī ||

Worshipped by gods, sages and priests, pure and glorious, O maker of day, wearing a garland of divine rays, you crush the forces of sin.

॥ ॐ जय कश्यप नन्दन ॥Om jai Kashyap nandan

सकल सुकर्म प्रसविता, सविता शुभकारी।
विश्व विलोचन मोचन, भव बन्धन भारी॥

Sakal sukarma prasavitā, savitā shubhkārī |
Vishva vilochan mochan, bhav bandhan bhārī ||

Savita, source of all good deeds and bringer of blessings, eye of the universe, you release us from the heavy bonds of worldly life.

॥ ॐ जय कश्यप नन्दन ॥Om jai Kashyap nandan

कमल समूह विकासक, नाशक त्रय तापा।
सेवत सहज हरत अति, मनसिज संतापा॥

Kamal samūh vikāsak, nāshak tray tāpā |
Sevat sahaj harat ati, mansij santāpā ||

You open the lotuses and destroy the three kinds of suffering; those who serve you are easily freed from the torment of desire.

॥ ॐ जय कश्यप नन्दन ॥Om jai Kashyap nandan

नेत्र व्याधि हर सुरवर, भू पीड़ा हारी।
वृष्टि विमोचन संतत, परहित व्रतधारी॥

Netra vyādhi har survar, bhū pīdā hārī |
Vrishti vimochan santat, parahit vratadhārī ||

Best of gods, you cure diseases of the eye and relieve the earth's pain; you release the rains and are ever devoted to the good of others.

॥ ॐ जय कश्यप नन्दन ॥Om jai Kashyap nandan

सूर्यदेव करुणाकर, अब करुणा कीजै।
हर अज्ञान मोह सब, तत्त्वज्ञान दीजै॥

Sūryadev karunākar, ab karunā kījai |
Har agyān moh sab, tattvagyān dījai ||

Merciful Surya Dev, show us mercy now: take away all ignorance and delusion and give us knowledge of the truth.

॥ ॐ जय कश्यप नन्दन ॥Om jai Kashyap nandan

The refrain ॥ ॐ जय कश्यप नन्दन ॥ is sung after every stanza, as marked.
