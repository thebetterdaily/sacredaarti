55 · SHLOKA

Achyutam Keshavam

अच्युतं केशवं

VishnuDeity
ShlokaForm
Daily, ThursdayDay
Ekadashi, JanmashtamiOccasion

The first verse of the Achyutashtakam, attributed to Adi Shankaracharya: a string of the names of Vishnu, Krishna and Rama. It is the basis of the much-loved bhajan 'Achyutam Keshavam Krishna Damodaram'.

अच्युतं केशवं रामनारायणं कृष्णदामोदरं वासुदेवं हरिम्।
श्रीधरं माधवं गोपिकावल्लभं जानकीनायकं रामचन्द्रं भजे॥

Achyutam keshavam rāmanārāyanam krishnadāmodaram vāsudevam harim |
Shrīdharam mādhavam gopikāvallabham jānakīnāyakam rāmachandram bhaje ||

I worship Achyuta the unfailing, Keshava, Rama and Narayana, Krishna and Damodara, Vasudeva and Hari; Shridhara, Madhava, the beloved of the gopis, and Ramachandra, the lord of Janaki.
