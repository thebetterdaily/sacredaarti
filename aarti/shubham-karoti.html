Shubham Karoti (Lighting the Lamp)

शुभं करोति कल्याणम्

UniversalDeity
ShlokaForm
DailyDay
Evening lampOccasion
ॐ

Recited when lighting the evening lamp at home; a favourite first prayer for children.

शुभं करोति कल्याणमारोग्यं धनसम्पदा।
शत्रुबुद्धिविनाशाय दीपज्योतिर्नमोऽस्तु ते॥

Shubham karoti kalyānamārogyam dhanasampadā |
Shatrubuddhivināshāya dīpajyotirnamo'stu te ||

Salutations to the light of the lamp, which brings goodness, well-being, health and prosperity, and destroys hostile thoughts.
