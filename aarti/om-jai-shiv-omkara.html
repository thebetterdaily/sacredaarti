Sacred Aarti / Shiva

06 · AARTI

Om Jai Shiv Omkara

ॐ जय शिव ओंकारा

ShivaDeity
AartiForm
MondayDay
Mahashivratri, Shravan MondaysOccasion

The principal aarti of Lord Shiva, sung on Mondays, in the month of Shravan and on Mahashivratri. It praises Brahma, Vishnu and Shiva as one reality within the sacred syllable Om, and closes with the signature of Swami Shivanand.

ॐ जय शिव ओंकारा, स्वामी जय शिव ओंकारा।
ब्रह्मा विष्णु सदाशिव, अर्द्धांगी धारा॥

Om jai Shiv onkārā, swāmī jai Shiv onkārā |
Brahmā Vishnu sadāshiv, arddhāngī dhārā ||

Glory to Shiva, the form of Om: Brahma, Vishnu and Sadashiva, who holds the Goddess as half of his own body.

एकानन चतुरानन पञ्चानन राजे।
हंसासन गरूड़ासन वृषवाहन साजे॥

Ekānan chaturānan panchānan rāje |
Hansāsan garūdāsan vrishvāhan sāje ||

With one face (Vishnu), four faces (Brahma) and five faces (Shiva) you shine, riding the swan, the eagle Garuda and the bull.

॥ ॐ जय शिव ओंकारा ॥Om jai Shiv onkārā

दो भुज चार चतुर्भुज दसभुज अति सोहे।
त्रिगुण रूप निरखते त्रिभुवन जन मोहे॥

Do bhuj chār chaturbhuj dasbhuj ati sohe |
Trigun rūp nirakhte tribhuvan jan mohe ||

With two, four and ten arms you look magnificent; seeing your threefold form, the people of the three worlds are enchanted.

॥ ॐ जय शिव ओंकारा ॥Om jai Shiv onkārā

अक्षमाला वनमाला मुण्डमाला धारी।
त्रिपुरारी कंसारी कर माला धारी॥

Akshamālā vanmālā mundamālā dhārī |
Tripurārī kansārī kar mālā dhārī ||

You wear a rosary of rudraksha, a garland of forest flowers and a garland of skulls; you are the destroyer of Tripura and of Kamsa, holding a mala in your hand.

॥ ॐ जय शिव ओंकारा ॥Om jai Shiv onkārā

श्वेताम्बर पीताम्बर बाघम्बर अंगे।
सनकादिक गरुड़ादिक भूतादिक संगे॥

Shvetāmbar pītāmbar bāghambar ange |
Sankādik garudādik bhūtādik sange ||

You are clad in white, in yellow and in tiger skin, accompanied by the sage Sanaka, by Garuda and by spirits.

॥ ॐ जय शिव ओंकारा ॥Om jai Shiv onkārā

कर के मध्य कमण्डलु चक्र त्रिशूलधारी।
सुखकारी दुखहारी जगपालन कारी॥

Kar ke madhya kamandalu chakra trishūldhārī |
Sukhkārī dukhhārī jagpālan kārī ||

In your hands are the water pot, the discus and the trident; you give joy, remove sorrow and sustain the world.

॥ ॐ जय शिव ओंकारा ॥Om jai Shiv onkārā

ब्रह्मा विष्णु सदाशिव जानत अविवेका।
प्रणवाक्षर में शोभित ये तीनों एका॥

Brahmā Vishnu sadāshiv jānat avivekā |
Pranavākshar mein shobhit ye tīnon ekā ||

Only the unwise think of Brahma, Vishnu and Sadashiva as separate; in the syllable Om the three shine as one.

॥ ॐ जय शिव ओंकारा ॥Om jai Shiv onkārā

लक्ष्मी व सावित्री पार्वती संगा।
पार्वती अर्द्धांगी शिवलहरी गंगा॥

Lakshmī va sāvitrī Pārvatī sangā |
Pārvatī arddhāngī shivlaharī Gangā ||

Lakshmi, Savitri and Parvati are with them; Parvati is Shiva's other half and the Ganga flows as his wave.

॥ ॐ जय शिव ओंकारा ॥Om jai Shiv onkārā

पर्वत सोहैं पार्वती, शंकर कैलासा।
भांग धतूर का भोजन, भस्मी में वासा॥

Parvat sohain Pārvatī, Shankar Kailāsā |
Bhāng dhatūr kā bhojan, bhasmī mein vāsā ||

Parvati graces the mountain and Shankar dwells on Kailash, taking bhang and datura and living amid sacred ash.

॥ ॐ जय शिव ओंकारा ॥Om jai Shiv onkārā

जटा में गंग बहत है, गल मुण्डन माला।
शेष नाग लिपटावत, ओढ़त मृगछाला॥

Jatā mein gang bahat hai, gal mundan mālā |
Shesh nāg liptāvat, odhat mrigchhālā ||

The Ganga flows in his matted hair, a garland of skulls hangs at his neck, the serpent Shesha coils around him and he wears a deerskin.

॥ ॐ जय शिव ओंकारा ॥Om jai Shiv onkārā

काशी में विराजे विश्वनाथ, नन्दी ब्रह्मचारी।
नित उठ दर्शन पावत, महिमा अति भारी॥

Kāshī mein virāje Vishvanāth, Nandī brahmachārī |
Nit uth darshan pāvat, mahimā ati bhārī ||

As Vishwanath he dwells in Kashi with Nandi, the celibate devotee; those who see him daily know his great glory.

॥ ॐ जय शिव ओंकारा ॥Om jai Shiv onkārā

त्रिगुणस्वामी जी की आरती, जो कोई नर गावे।
कहत शिवानन्द स्वामी, मनवांछित फल पावे॥

Trigunswāmī jī kī ārtī, jo koī nar gāve |
Kahat Shivānand swāmī, manvānchhit phal pāve ||

Swami Shivanand says: whoever sings the aarti of the Lord of the three qualities receives the fruit of their heart's wish.

॥ ॐ जय शिव ओंकारा ॥Om jai Shiv onkārā

The refrain ॥ ॐ जय शिव ओंकारा ॥ is sung after every stanza, as marked.
