Sacred Aarti / Shri Krishna / Mantra

36 · MANTRA

Hare Krishna Mahamantra

हरे कृष्ण महामन्त्र

Shri KrishnaDeity
MantraForm
Daily, EkadashiDay
Janmashtami, EkadashiOccasion

The sixteen-word mantra of the names of Hari, Shri Krishna and Shri Ram, found in the Kali Mata-Santarana Upanishad and made famous worldwide by Chaitanya Mahaprabhu and later ISKCON. It is chanted on a japa mala (traditionally 108 times per round) and sung in kirtan. The Upanishad gives the 'Hare Rama' half first; kirtan usually begins with 'Hare Krishna'.

हरे कृष्ण हरे कृष्ण कृष्ण कृष्ण हरे हरे।
हरे राम हरे राम राम राम हरे हरे॥

Hare Krishna hare Krishna Krishna Krishna hare hare |
Hare rāma hare rāma rāma rāma hare hare ||

O Hari (Radha Rani, the divine energy), O Shri Krishna, O Shri Ram: the mantra simply calls the names of God with love, asking to be engaged in his service.
