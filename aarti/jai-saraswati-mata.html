Sacred Aarti / Saraswati Mata / Aarti

29 · AARTI

Jai Saraswati Mata

जय सरस्वती माता

Saraswati MataDeity
AartiForm
ThursdayDay
Vasant Panchami, Exams and new studiesOccasion

Sung on Vasant Panchami, at the start of a child's schooling and before exams. Saraswati Mata is the goddess of learning, music and speech; the aarti asks her to remove ignorance and grant knowledge.

जय सरस्वती माता, मैया जय सरस्वती माता।
सदगुण वैभव शालिनी, त्रिभुवन विख्याता॥

Jai Saraswatī mātā, maiyā jai Saraswatī mātā |
Sadgun vaibhav shālinī, tribhuvan vikhyātā ||

Glory to Saraswati Mata, rich in every virtue and famed in the three worlds.

चन्द्रवदनि पद्मासिनि, द्युति मंगलकारी।
सोहे शुभ हंस सवारी, अतुल तेजधारी॥

Chandravadani padmāsini, dyuti mangalkārī |
Sohe shubh hans savārī, atul tejdhārī ||

Moon-faced one seated on a lotus, your radiance is auspicious; you ride a graceful swan and shine with matchless brilliance.

॥ ॐ जय सरस्वती माता ॥Om jai Saraswatī mātā

बाएँ कर में वीणा, दाएँ कर माला।
शीश मुकुट मणि सोहे, गल मोतियन माला॥

Bāen kar mein vīnā, dāen kar mālā |
Shīsh mukut mani sohe, gal motiyan mālā ||

A veena is in your left hand and a rosary in your right; a jewelled crown adorns your head and pearls your neck.

॥ ॐ जय सरस्वती माता ॥Om jai Saraswatī mātā

देवी शरण जो आए, उनका उद्धार किया।
पैठी मंथरा दासी, रावण संहार किया॥

Devī sharan jo āe, unkā uddhār kiyā |
Paithī Mantharā dāsī, Rāvan sanhār kiyā ||

O Goddess, you uplift all who come to you. You entered the mind of the maid Manthara, setting in motion the events that led to Ravana's end.

॥ ॐ जय सरस्वती माता ॥Om jai Saraswatī mātā

विद्या ज्ञान प्रदायिनि, ज्ञान प्रकाश भरो।
मोह अज्ञान और तिमिर का, जग से नाश करो॥

Vidyā gyān pradāyini, gyān prakāsh bharo |
Moh agyān aur timir kā, jag se nāsh karo ||

Giver of learning and wisdom, fill us with the light of knowledge, and destroy delusion, ignorance and darkness in the world.

॥ ॐ जय सरस्वती माता ॥Om jai Saraswatī mātā

धूप दीप फल मेवा, माँ स्वीकार करो।
ज्ञानचक्षु दे माता, जग निस्तार करो॥

Dhūp dīp phal mevā, mān swīkār karo |
Gyānchakshu de mātā, jag nistār karo ||

Mother, accept our incense, lamp, fruits and dry fruits; give us the eye of wisdom and lift the world across.

॥ ॐ जय सरस्वती माता ॥Om jai Saraswatī mātā

माँ सरस्वती की आरती, जो कोई जन गावे।
हितकारी सुखकारी, ज्ञान भक्ति पावे॥

Mān Saraswatī kī ārtī, jo koī jan gāve |
Hitkārī sukhkārī, gyān bhakti pāve ||

Whoever sings Saraswati Mata's aarti gains wisdom and devotion, which bring both well-being and happiness.

॥ ॐ जय सरस्वती माता ॥Om jai Saraswatī mātā

The refrain ॥ ॐ जय सरस्वती माता ॥ is sung after every stanza, as marked.
