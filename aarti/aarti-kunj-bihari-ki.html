Sacred Aarti / Krishna

08 · AARTI

Aarti Kunj Bihari Ki

आरती कुंजबिहारी की

KrishnaDeity
AartiForm
WednesdayDay
Janmashtami, HoliOccasion

The best-known aarti of Krishna as Kunj Bihari, the one who roams the groves of Vrindavan. Its long, rhythmic stanzas paint Krishna with his flute, peacock crown and Radha beside him; it is the centrepiece of Janmashtami night.

आरती कुंजबिहारी की, श्री गिरिधर कृष्ण मुरारी की॥

Ārtī Kunjbihārī kī, shrī Giridhar Krishna Murārī kī ||

Perform the aarti of Kunj Bihari, of Shri Krishna, lifter of the mountain and slayer of the demon Mura.

गले में बैजंती माला, बजावै मुरली मधुर बाला।
श्रवण में कुण्डल झलकाला, नंद के आनंद नंदलाला॥
गगन सम अंग कांति काली, राधिका चमक रही आली।
लतन में ठाढ़े बनमाली॥
भ्रमर सी अलक, कस्तूरी तिलक, चंद्र सी झलक,
ललित छवि श्यामा प्यारी की॥

Gale mein baijantī mālā, bajāvai murlī madhur bālā |
Shravan mein kundal jhalkālā, nand ke ānand nandlālā ||
Gagan sam ang kānti kālī, Rādhikā chamak rahī ālī |
Latan mein thādhe banmālī ||
Bhramar sī alak, kastūrī tilak, chandra sī jhalak,
Lalit chhavi shyāmā pyārī kī ||

A Vaijayanti garland at his neck, the young boy plays sweet music on his flute; earrings glitter at his ears, the darling of Nanda. His body is dark as the sky, and Radha shines beside him; the one with the forest garland stands among the vines. Curls like black bees, a musk tilak, a moon-like glow: lovely is the sight of him with his beloved Radha.

॥ आरती कुंजबिहारी की, श्री गिरिधर कृष्ण मुरारी की ॥Ārtī Kunjbihārī kī, shrī Giridhar Krishna Murārī kī

कनकमय मोर मुकुट बिलसै, देवता दरसन को तरसैं।
गगन सों सुमन रासि बरसै॥
बजे मुरचंग, मधुर मिरदंग, ग्वालिन संग,
अतुल रति गोप कुमारी की॥

Kanakmay mor mukut bilsai, devtā darsan ko tarsain |
Gagan son suman rāsi barsai ||
Baje murchang, madhur mirdang, gvālin sang,
Atul rati gop kumārī kī ||

His golden peacock crown sparkles, and the gods long for a glimpse of him as flowers shower from the sky. The mouth-harp and sweet mridanga play as he dances with the cowherd girls, whose love for him is beyond compare.

॥ आरती कुंजबिहारी की, श्री गिरिधर कृष्ण मुरारी की ॥Ārtī Kunjbihārī kī, shrī Giridhar Krishna Murārī kī

जहाँ ते प्रकट भई गंगा, सकल मन हारिणि श्री गंगा।
स्मरन ते होत मोह भंगा॥
बसी शिव सीस, जटा के बीच, हरै अघ कीच,
चरन छवि श्रीबनवारी की॥

Jahān te prakat bhaī Gangā, sakal man hārini shrī Gangā |
Smaran te hot moh bhangā ||
Basī Shiv sīs, jatā ke bīch, harai agh kīch,
Charan chhavi Shrī Banvārī kī ||

From his feet the Ganga appeared, the holy river that captivates every heart; remembering her breaks the spell of delusion. She lives in Shiva's matted hair and washes away the mire of sin: such is the beauty of the feet of Shri Banwari.

॥ आरती कुंजबिहारी की, श्री गिरिधर कृष्ण मुरारी की ॥Ārtī Kunjbihārī kī, shrī Giridhar Krishna Murārī kī

चमकती उज्ज्वल तट रेनू, बज रही वृंदावन बेनू।
चहुँ दिसि गोपि ग्वाल धेनू॥
हँसत मृदु मंद, चाँदनी चंद, कटत भव फंद,
टेर सुन दीन दुखारी की॥

Chamaktī ujjval tat renū, baj rahī Vrindāvan benū |
Chahun disi gopi gvāl dhenū ||
Hansat mridu mand, chāndanī chand, katat bhav phand,
Ter sun dīn dukhārī kī ||

The bright sands of the Yamuna glitter and his flute sounds through Vrindavan, with gopis, cowherds and cows all around. He smiles softly, like moonlight, and cuts the noose of worldly life when he hears the cry of the humble and suffering.

॥ आरती कुंजबिहारी की, श्री गिरिधर कृष्ण मुरारी की ॥Ārtī Kunjbihārī kī, shrī Giridhar Krishna Murārī kī

The refrain ॥ आरती कुंजबिहारी की, श्री गिरिधर कृष्ण मुरारी की ॥ is sung after every stanza, as marked.
