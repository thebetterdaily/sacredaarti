39 · SHLOKA

Kshama Prarthana (Prayer for Forgiveness)

क्षमा प्रार्थना

UniversalDeity
ShlokaForm
DailyDay
End of pujaOccasion
ॐ

Recited at the very end of a puja, asking forgiveness for anything done imperfectly. It reminds us that devotion matters more than ritual precision.

आवाहनं न जानामि न जानामि विसर्जनम्।
पूजां चैव न जानामि क्षमस्व परमेश्वर॥
मन्त्रहीनं क्रियाहीनं भक्तिहीनं सुरेश्वर।
यत्पूजितं मया देव परिपूर्णं तदस्तु मे॥

Āvāhanam na jānāmi na jānāmi visarjanam |
Pūjām chaiva na jānāmi kshamasva parameshvara ||
Mantrahīnam kriyāhīnam bhaktihīnam sureshvara |
Yatpūjitam mayā deva paripūrnam tadastu me ||

I do not know how to invite you, nor how to bid you farewell, nor how to worship you properly: forgive me, Supreme Lord. Whatever worship I have offered, lacking in mantra, in ritual and in devotion, O Lord of the gods, may it be made complete.
