Sacred Aarti / Ganesha

33 · SHLOKA

Gajananam Bhutaganadi Sevitam

गजाननं भूतगणादि सेवितं

GaneshaDeity
ShlokaForm
Daily, WednesdayDay
Ganesh ChaturthiOccasion

A traditional verse of salutation to Ganesha, widely chanted at the start of morning prayers.

गजाननं भूतगणादि सेवितं कपित्थ जम्बू फलचारु भक्षणम्।
उमासुतं शोक विनाशकारकं नमामि विघ्नेश्वर पादपङ्कजम्॥

Gajānanam bhūtaganādi sevitam kapittha jambū phalachāru bhakshanam |
Umāsutam shoka vināshakārakam namāmi vighneshvara pādapankajam ||

I bow to the lotus feet of Vighneshvara, the elephant-faced one served by Shiva's hosts, who enjoys wood-apple and jamun fruit; the son of Uma, who destroys sorrow.
