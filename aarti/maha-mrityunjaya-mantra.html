17 · MANTRA

Maha Mrityunjaya Mantra

महामृत्युञ्जय मन्त्र

ShivaDeity
MantraForm
Monday, DailyDay
Mahashivratri, Times of illness or fearOccasion

A Vedic mantra from the Rig Veda (7.59.12), addressed to Shiva as Tryambaka, the three-eyed one. It is chanted for healing, protection and freedom from the fear of death, traditionally 108 times on a rudraksha mala.

ॐ त्र्यम्बकं यजामहे सुगन्धिं पुष्टिवर्धनम्।
उर्वारुकमिव बन्धनान्मृत्योर्मुक्षीय माऽमृतात्॥

Om tryambakam yajāmahe sugandhim pushtivardhanam |
Urvārukamiva bandhanānmrityormukshīya mā'mritāt ||

We worship the three-eyed one, who is fragrant and who nourishes all beings. As a ripe cucumber is freed from its vine, may we be freed from death, and not from immortality.
