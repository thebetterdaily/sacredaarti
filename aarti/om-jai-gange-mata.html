20 · AARTI

Om Jai Gange Mata

ॐ जय गंगे माता

GangaDeity
AartiForm
Daily PujaDay
Ganga Dussehra, Kartik PurnimaOccasion

Sung at the banks of the Ganga in Haridwar, Rishikesh and Varanasi each evening, and at home on Ganga Dussehra. It recalls how the river came to earth to liberate the sons of King Sagar.

ॐ जय गंगे माता, श्री जय गंगे माता।
जो नर तुमको ध्याता, मनवांछित फल पाता॥

Om jai Gange mātā, shrī jai Gange mātā |
Jo nar tumko dhyātā, manvānchhit phal pātā ||

Glory to Mother Ganga; whoever meditates on you receives the fruit of their heart's wish.

चन्द्र सी ज्योति तुम्हारी, जल निर्मल आता।
शरण पड़े जो तेरी, सो नर तर जाता॥

Chandra sī jyoti tumhārī, jal nirmal ātā |
Sharan pade jo terī, so nar tar jātā ||

Your light is like the moon and your waters flow pure; whoever takes refuge in you is carried across.

॥ ॐ जय गंगे माता ॥Om jai Gange mātā

पुत्र सगर के तारे, सब जग को ज्ञाता।
कृपा दृष्टि तुम्हारी, त्रिभुवन सुख दाता॥

Putra Sagar ke tāre, sab jag ko gyātā |
Kripā drishti tumhārī, tribhuvan sukh dātā ||

You liberated the sons of King Sagar, as the whole world knows; your gracious glance brings happiness to the three worlds.

॥ ॐ जय गंगे माता ॥Om jai Gange mātā

एक ही बार जो तेरी, शरणागति आता।
यम की त्रास मिटाकर, परम गति पाता॥

Ek hī bār jo terī, sharnāgti ātā |
Yam kī trās mitākar, param gati pātā ||

Whoever comes to your shelter even once is freed from the fear of death and reaches the highest state.

॥ ॐ जय गंगे माता ॥Om jai Gange mātā

आरती मात तुम्हारी, जो जन नित गाता।
दास वही सहज में, मुक्ति को पाता॥

Ārtī māt tumhārī, jo jan nit gātā |
Dās vahī sahaj mein, mukti ko pātā ||

The devotee who sings your aarti every day easily attains liberation.

॥ ॐ जय गंगे माता ॥Om jai Gange mātā

The refrain ॥ ॐ जय गंगे माता ॥ is sung after every stanza, as marked.
