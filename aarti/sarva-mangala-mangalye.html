Sacred Aarti / Durga Mata / Shloka

42 · SHLOKA

Sarva Mangala Mangalye

सर्वमङ्गलमाङ्गल्ये

Durga MataDeity
ShlokaForm
Friday, DailyDay
Navratri, WeddingsOccasion

From the Devi Mahatmya (chapter 11). It is one of the most widely chanted verses to the Goddess, and is often recited at weddings and auspicious beginnings.

सर्वमङ्गलमाङ्गल्ये शिवे सर्वार्थसाधिके।
शरण्ये त्र्यम्बके गौरि नारायणि नमोऽस्तु ते॥

Sarvamangalamāngalye shive sarvārthasādhike |
Sharanye tryambake gauri nārāyani namo'stu te ||

O auspiciousness of all that is auspicious, gracious one, who fulfils every aim, our refuge, three-eyed Gauri, Narayani: salutations to you.
