Aarti Shri Sai Guruvar Ki

आरती श्री साईं गुरुवर की

Sai BabaDeity
AartiForm
ThursdayDay
Thursday, Guru PurnimaOccasion

A Hindi aarti to Sai Baba of Shirdi, sung especially on Thursdays (Guruvar). It speaks of his udi (sacred ash), of devotees of every faith who came to Shirdi, and closes with the signature 'Saidas'.

आरती श्री साईं गुरुवर की।
परमानन्द सदा सुरवर की॥

Ārtī shrī Sāīn guruvar kī |
Parmānand sadā survar kī ||

Perform the aarti of the revered Guru Sai, who is supreme bliss and ever the best among the divine.

जा की कृपा विपुल सुखकारी।
दुःख शोक संकट भयहारी॥

Jā kī kripā vipul sukhkārī |
Dukh shok sankat bhayhārī ||

His abundant grace brings happiness and removes sorrow, grief, trouble and fear.

॥ आरती श्री साईं गुरुवर की, परमानन्द सदा सुरवर की ॥Ārtī shrī Sāīn guruvar kī, parmānand sadā survar kī

शिरडी में अवतार रचाया।
चमत्कार से तत्त्व दिखाया॥

Shirdī mein avtār rachāyā |
Chamatkār se tattva dikhāyā ||

He took birth (as a saint) in Shirdi and revealed the truth through his miracles.

॥ आरती श्री साईं गुरुवर की, परमानन्द सदा सुरवर की ॥Ārtī shrī Sāīn guruvar kī, parmānand sadā survar kī

कितने भक्त चरण पर आए।
वे सुख शान्ति चिरन्तन पाए॥

Kitne bhakt charan par āe |
Ve sukh shānti chirantan pāe ||

Countless devotees came to his feet and found lasting happiness and peace.

॥ आरती श्री साईं गुरुवर की, परमानन्द सदा सुरवर की ॥Ārtī shrī Sāīn guruvar kī, parmānand sadā survar kī

भाव धरै जो मन में जैसा।
पावत अनुभव वो ही वैसा॥

Bhāv dharai jo man mein jaisā |
Pāvat anubhav vo hī vaisā ||

Whatever feeling one holds in the heart, that is the experience one receives.

॥ आरती श्री साईं गुरुवर की, परमानन्द सदा सुरवर की ॥Ārtī shrī Sāīn guruvar kī, parmānand sadā survar kī

गुरु की उदी लगावे तन को।
समाधान लाभत उस मन को॥

Guru kī udī lagāve tan ko |
Samādhān lābhat us man ko ||

Whoever applies the Guru's udi (sacred ash) to the body finds contentment of mind.

॥ आरती श्री साईं गुरुवर की, परमानन्द सदा सुरवर की ॥Ārtī shrī Sāīn guruvar kī, parmānand sadā survar kī

साईं नाम सदा जो गावे।
सो फल जग में शाश्वत पावे॥

Sāīn nām sadā jo gāve |
So phal jag mein shāshvat pāve ||

Whoever always sings the name of Sai gains an everlasting reward in this world.

॥ आरती श्री साईं गुरुवर की, परमानन्द सदा सुरवर की ॥Ārtī shrī Sāīn guruvar kī, parmānand sadā survar kī

गुरुवासर करि पूजा सेवा।
उस पर कृपा करत गुरुदेवा॥

Guruvāsar kari pūjā sevā |
Us par kripā karat gurudevā ||

On those who worship and serve on Thursday, the Guru showers his grace.

॥ आरती श्री साईं गुरुवर की, परमानन्द सदा सुरवर की ॥Ārtī shrī Sāīn guruvar kī, parmānand sadā survar kī

राम कृष्ण हनुमान रूप में।
दे दर्शन जानत जो मन में॥

Rām Krishna Hanumān rūp mein |
De darshan jānat jo man mein ||

He appears as Rama, Krishna or Hanuman to each devotee, knowing what is in their heart.

॥ आरती श्री साईं गुरुवर की, परमानन्द सदा सुरवर की ॥Ārtī shrī Sāīn guruvar kī, parmānand sadā survar kī

विविध धर्म के सेवक आते।
दर्शन कर इच्छित फल पाते॥

Vividh dharm ke sevak āte |
Darshan kar ichchhit phal pāte ||

Followers of many faiths come to him, and through his darshan receive what they desire.

॥ आरती श्री साईं गुरुवर की, परमानन्द सदा सुरवर की ॥Ārtī shrī Sāīn guruvar kī, parmānand sadā survar kī

जै बोलो साईं बाबा की।
जै बोलो अवधूत गुरु की॥

Jai bolo Sāīn bābā kī |
Jai bolo avdhūt guru kī ||

Hail Sai Baba! Hail the Guru who is free of all worldly ties!

॥ आरती श्री साईं गुरुवर की, परमानन्द सदा सुरवर की ॥Ārtī shrī Sāīn guruvar kī, parmānand sadā survar kī

'साईंदास' आरती को गावे।
घर में बसि सुख मंगल पावे॥

'Sāīndās' ārtī ko gāve |
Ghar mein basi sukh mangal pāve ||

Saidas sings this aarti: may happiness and blessings dwell in the home of all who sing it.

॥ आरती श्री साईं गुरुवर की, परमानन्द सदा सुरवर की ॥Ārtī shrī Sāīn guruvar kī, parmānand sadā survar kī

The refrain ॥ आरती श्री साईं गुरुवर की, परमानन्द सदा सुरवर की ॥ is sung after every stanza, as marked.
