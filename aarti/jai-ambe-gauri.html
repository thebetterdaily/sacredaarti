Sacred Aarti / Durga

07 · AARTI

Jai Ambe Gauri

जय अम्बे गौरी

DurgaDeity
AartiForm
Friday, TuesdayDay
Navratri, Durga PujaOccasion

The most popular aarti of Goddess Durga, sung every evening during both Navratris. It describes her beauty, her weapons and her victories over the demons Shumbha, Nishumbha, Mahishasura, Chanda, Munda and Raktabija, as told in the Devi Mahatmya.

जय अम्बे गौरी, मैया जय श्यामा गौरी।
तुमको निशदिन ध्यावत, हरि ब्रह्मा शिवरी॥

Jai Ambe Gaurī, maiyā jai shyāmā Gaurī |
Tumko nishdin dhyāvat, Hari Brahmā shivrī ||

Glory to Mother Ambe, fair Gauri and dark Shyama, on whom Vishnu, Brahma and Shiva meditate day and night.

माँग सिन्दूर विराजत, टीको मृगमद को।
उज्ज्वल से दोउ नैना, चन्द्रवदन नीको॥

Māng sindūr virājat, tīko mrigmad ko |
Ujjval se dou nainā, chandravadan nīko ||

Vermilion shines in your hair parting with a mark of musk on your brow; your two eyes are bright and your face is lovely as the moon.

॥ ॐ जय अम्बे गौरी ॥Om jai Ambe Gaurī

कनक समान कलेवर, रक्ताम्बर राजै।
रक्तपुष्प गल माला, कण्ठन पर साजै॥

Kanak samān kalevar, raktāmbar rājai |
Raktapushp gal mālā, kanthan par sājai ||

Your body glows like gold and you are dressed in red; a garland of red flowers adorns your neck.

॥ ॐ जय अम्बे गौरी ॥Om jai Ambe Gaurī

केहरि वाहन राजत, खड्ग खप्पर धारी।
सुर नर मुनिजन सेवत, तिनके दुखहारी॥

Kehari vāhan rājat, khadg khappar dhārī |
Sur nar munijan sevat, tinke dukhhārī ||

You ride a lion and hold a sword and a skull-bowl; gods, people and sages serve you, and you take away their sorrows.

॥ ॐ जय अम्बे गौरी ॥Om jai Ambe Gaurī

कानन कुण्डल शोभित, नासाग्रे मोती।
कोटिक चन्द्र दिवाकर, राजत सम ज्योती॥

Kānan kundal shobhit, nāsāgre motī |
Kotik chandra divākar, rājat sam jyotī ||

Earrings shine at your ears and a pearl at your nose; your radiance equals millions of moons and suns.

॥ ॐ जय अम्बे गौरी ॥Om jai Ambe Gaurī

शुम्भ निशुम्भ बिदारे, महिषासुर घाती।
धूम्र विलोचन नैना, निशदिन मदमाती॥

Shumbh nishumbh bidāre, Mahishāsur ghātī |
Dhūmra vilochan nainā, nishdin madmātī ||

You tore apart Shumbha and Nishumbha and slew Mahishasura; your eyes destroyed Dhumralochana, and you are ever intoxicated with divine joy.

॥ ॐ जय अम्बे गौरी ॥Om jai Ambe Gaurī

चण्ड मुण्ड संहारे, शोणित बीज हरे।
मधु कैटभ दोउ मारे, सुर भयहीन करे॥

Chand mund sanhāre, shonit bīj hare |
Madhu kaitabh dou māre, sur bhayahīn kare ||

You destroyed Chanda and Munda and ended Raktabija; you slew Madhu and Kaitabha and freed the gods from fear.

॥ ॐ जय अम्बे गौरी ॥Om jai Ambe Gaurī

ब्रह्माणी रुद्राणी, तुम कमला रानी।
आगम निगम बखानी, तुम शिव पटरानी॥

Brahmānī rudrānī, tum kamlā rānī |
Āgam nigam bakhānī, tum Shiv patrānī ||

You are Brahmani and Rudrani, you are Queen Kamala (Lakshmi); the scriptures praise you, and you are Shiva's chief queen.

॥ ॐ जय अम्बे गौरी ॥Om jai Ambe Gaurī

चौंसठ योगिनी गावत, नृत्य करत भैरों।
बाजत ताल मृदंगा, अरु बाजत डमरू॥

Chaunsath yoginī gāvat, nritya karat bhairon |
Bājat tāl mridangā, aru bājat damrū ||

The sixty-four yoginis sing and Bhairava dances, to the beat of cymbals, mridanga and damru.

॥ ॐ जय अम्बे गौरी ॥Om jai Ambe Gaurī

तुम ही जग की माता, तुम ही हो भरता।
भक्तन की दुख हरता, सुख सम्पति करता॥

Tum hī jag kī mātā, tum hī ho bhartā |
Bhaktan kī dukh hartā, sukh sampati kartā ||

You are the mother of the world and also its sustainer; you remove the sorrows of devotees and grant them happiness and prosperity.

॥ ॐ जय अम्बे गौरी ॥Om jai Ambe Gaurī

भुजा चार अति शोभित, वर मुद्रा धारी।
मनवांछित फल पावत, सेवत नर नारी॥

Bhujā chār ati shobhit, var mudrā dhārī |
Manvānchhit phal pāvat, sevat nar nārī ||

Your four arms are beautiful, one raised in blessing; the men and women who serve you receive their heart's wish.

॥ ॐ जय अम्बे गौरी ॥Om jai Ambe Gaurī

कंचन थाल विराजत, अगर कपूर बाती।
श्रीमालकेतु में राजत, कोटि रतन ज्योती॥

Kanchan thāl virājat, agar kapūr bātī |
Shrīmālketu mein rājat, koti ratan jyotī ||

A golden plate is set with incense, camphor and wicks; in Shri Malketu you shine with the light of countless gems.

॥ ॐ जय अम्बे गौरी ॥Om jai Ambe Gaurī

श्री अम्बेजी की आरती, जो कोई नर गावे।
कहत शिवानन्द स्वामी, सुख सम्पति पावे॥

Shrī ambejī kī ārtī, jo koī nar gāve |
Kahat Shivānand swāmī, sukh sampati pāve ||

Swami Shivanand says: whoever sings this aarti of Mother Ambe gains happiness and prosperity.

॥ ॐ जय अम्बे गौरी ॥Om jai Ambe Gaurī

The refrain ॥ ॐ जय अम्बे गौरी ॥ is sung after every stanza, as marked.
