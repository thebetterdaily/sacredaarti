What makes it different

  • Standard, complete texts, with the refrain marked exactly where it is sung.
  • Three layers for every stanza: Devanagari, a pronunciation-friendly Roman transliteration, and a plain-English meaning.
  • A short note on the origin of each aarti: who wrote it, and when and why it is sung.
  • A home puja guide, a weekday and festival finder, and a section of daily prayers for children.
  • Large, clear type laid out to be read while holding the aarti thali.
